ECOLOGICAL TEXTS 

The striking thing: three independent texts — a French essay on bricolage vs engineering, a 2025 book on ecologies of experimental cinema, and one on expanded animation — all circle the same idea, which happens to be the app's literal name: persistence of vision, the afterimage, the interval between frames as the real material. They also independently validate your guardrails (matte, restraint, decay-over-bloom, the détourned/curated apparatus). So the headline isn't "add features" — it's that the concept is being confirmed from three directions, and there's a signature family sitting unbuilt.

Here are the 10, strongest first. Tags: [NEW build], [reinforces concept], [extends X].

1. The afterimage family — earn the name «Palinopsia». [NEW build] All three texts. Jacobs' "Eternalism" — "unfrozen slices of time… going nowhere, without discernable start and stop" (thouvenel p66); Sherwin/McClure's phase-drift twins — two identical loops of 200 vs 199 frames that "shift in and out of sync… chaotic periods before shifting into sync" (expanim p170); Goethe's complement — a removed bright form leaves its space "much darker" (expanim p63). These are one shared primitive: a frame-history ring buffer read with a per-pixel/per-region temporal offset. Build it once → you get Eternalism (two frames N apart oscillating across a black interval), Phase-Drift Twin (A & B from the same source at rates R and R±ε, beating in and out of lock), and Afterimage (a slow-decaying negative of recent frames blooming back on a cut). This is the most on-brand thing in 750 pages.

2. Pulfrich depth — monochrome 3D, no anaglyph. [NEW build / extends Context] Thouvenel p67. "Dark stimuli are perceived a little later than light stimuli… providing two distinct images… merged to create a third dimension." A luminance-keyed per-pixel frame-delay (read from the same ring buffer as #1) gives real depth on any lateral motion with zero colour fringing — a near-black-safe companion to your anaglyph stage.

3. Homeostat modulator — the instrument self-stabilizes. [NEW build] Expnature p166–170, Welsby. Wind drove his camera speed; the system held "homeostatic" equilibrium. Build a negative-feedback modulator that watches an output property (frame energy, edge density, motion) and nudges parameters back toward a setpoint — your feedback-engine AGC promoted to a first-class modulator that parks the whole rig at edge-of-chaos instead of drifting to black or blow-out.

4. Buried / Weathered — slow entropy across a whole set. [NEW build / extends Cameraless] Expnature p91 (Lefrant), thouvenel p14 (reticulation). Film buried in soil so "biochemical breakdown… animates the figurative image." A time-integrated corrosion mask (reaction-diffusion) that monotonically worsens over performance time — eating luminance, blooming mineral colour along its fronts — with an "exhume/harvest" reset. Deliberately not a per-frame flicker; a decay that accumulates like real burial. Plus a reticulation émulsion texture (thermal crackle).

5. Compression-as-material: actant triggers on Datamosh. [extends Datamosh] Expnature p267–281, Perconte. An insect near the lens "leaves strokes of white pixels that winds… slowly fill again"; a leaf "creates blocks of grey pixels that persist and follow the wind." Add a sparse event-trigger input to your Datamosh node that injects localized persistent pixel-blocks which then advect along the existing optical-flow field and bleed colour into neighbours. Plus his "manifestation" mode: a new source doesn't cut, it completes itself out of the retained previous frame.

6. Flow ↔ Interruption — one temperament axis. [NEW build, unifies existing] Expanim p45, Sharits. Film is both vector (the continuous thread) and modular (the shutter chopping it). One macro knob, sibling to Tonicity/Shutter/Drift, that crossfades the entire instrument: toward Flow → motion-smoothing, echo, feedback continuity; toward Interruption → frame-hold, decimate, splice, strobe, blank frames. It gives a single expressive spine to half your finalizer tools.

7. Consume / Reagent — a blend mode where A and B etch each other. [NEW build] Expnature p120, Doing. A plant as chemical "second developer" so "both renditions consume each other," motion "between fight and embrace." Not a mix or wipe — a coupled reaction-diffusion where bright A eats into B and vice-versa and the boundary migrates over time. A genuinely new A/B relation.

8. Frame-Weave — a Sequencer that works at the single frame. [NEW build / reframes Sequencer] Thouvenel p39, Rose Lowder. She composed in-camera frame by frame, tracked on "a visual score… columns, each 24 images = one second," placing "spots of colour anywhere on the surface." A Sequencer mode that interleaves sources on a per-frame lattice (not blends), with a paintable 24-cells-per-second score grid. Temporal interlace is a cinematic register nothing in the app currently does.

9. Time-Lapse / Decimate — reveal motion invisible at 1:1. [NEW build] Expanim p95–99, Woodman. A two-screen piece: real-time left vs 1-frame-per-10-seconds right, where weeds "static in the left… agitate vigorously" in the right. A sample-and-hold-every-N temporal FX; preset it as same source, two rates across A/B to reproduce the control-vs-lapse revelation.

10. Burial→Exhumation macro-arc + the Long-Take/Veil discipline. [NEW build / reinforces concept] Expnature p53 (Fog Line: 11-min single shot, fog imperceptibly clearing), p233 (Los Ingrávidos). Two Sequencer long-forms: a dramatic Burial→Exhumation curve (slowly degrade the composite to near-illegibility, then recover), and its opposite — a "Veil" / slowness governor that forbids cuts and drives one imperceptibly-slow parameter over minutes. The antidote to your restless macro-forms.

Honourable mentions I left off the 10: a "lines-of-force" incrustation FX that cuts along the image's own gradients (thouvenel p23, distinct from Autocutter's rectangles); a "Metamorph" birth-from-within shape generator (expanim p120, Blu's Muto); complementary aperture-gate masks with a defocus↔flicker coupling (expanim p172); a "Void / edge-dissolve" Context control that makes emptiness the subject.

What Nuvotion is
A free, web-based, beginner-first VJ app (app.nuvotion.live) for audio-reactive live visuals. Big numbers up front: 82 effects, 43 transitions, 465 Milkdrop presets, 59 blend modes, every parameter syncable to BPM/bass/mid/treble, plus X/Y pad, motion tracking, strobe, layer keying/masking, A/B crossfader, playlists, MIDI/keyboard mapping, recording. Their charter is open-source, anti-blockchain/scraping, accessibility-over-profit, "craft not move-fast." Their GitHub also has real glitch tooling — ffmosh (an ffglitch fork), JPEGGED (true JPEG databending), a Formation cross-platform UI kit, and Harmony, an in-app AI assistant.

The core differences
Nuvotion	Palinopsia
Platform	Web browser (WebGL, butterchurn/Milkdrop)	Electron desktop (ISF, Spout/NDI, 2nd-display, projection warp)
Audience	Mass, beginner, "you can't mess this up"	A personal, opinionated instrument tied to your research + the Pandore/dataFLOU ecosystem
Aesthetic	Anything-goes VJ, incl. Milkdrop psychedelia	Disciplined doctrine — matte, near-black, one accent, glitch-as-controlled-texture, anti-kaleidoscope/plasma
Control	Self-contained (MIDI, keyboard, X/Y, audio)	OSC-first, played by an external brain, and now bidirectional (vision bus → OSC)
Model	Layers + FX + scenes + huge preset libraries	A deep instrument: modulators + Meta macros, coupling engine, Worlds/diegesis, temperament, generative macro-form sequencer, native nodes, monocular depth
The short version: Nuvotion is broad and accessible; Palinopsia is deep and opinionated. They chase reach and a low floor; you chase a high ceiling and a specific artistic voice. Notably, their headline 465 Milkdrop presets are exactly the register your guardrails forbid — so their biggest number is the least borrowable thing.

Features genuinely worth borrowing (that fit your doctrine)
X/Y performance pad(s) — the strongest fit. You have 16 Meta knobs but no 2-D touch surface. One or more X/Y pads, each mapping two Meta knobs/params to a draggable square, is a superb live gesture and pure UX (guardrail-neutral).
Setlist / Playlist — an ordered, manual scene list you step through on stage (next/prev, optional auto-advance), distinct from your generative macro-form sequencer. You have the scene bank (1–9) and the auto-pilot; a linear setlist is the missing performance middle-ground.
Per-layer masking — luma / gradient / shape masks (not just blend modes + keyer). This is real compositing depth you don't fully have, and it stays on-brand (a matte can be as restrained as you like).
One-click "react to bass/mid/treble" — your modulation is more powerful but takes setup. A quick affordance on any slider ("bind to bass," "bind to BPM") would give the instant audio-reactivity their beginners love, without diluting the modulator system.
Saveable UI workspaces — they emphasize custom resizable layouts. You have resizable panels; saving named layouts (perform / edit / minimal) is a nice touch.
(Against-doctrine, optional) butterchurn/Milkdrop as a tamed source — you could host Milkdrop as one optional generator and force it through Vibe/Context so it reads matte, but it fights the whole aesthetic. I'd skip it, or only allow it heavily graded.
(Speculative) An AI copilot like their Harmony — natural-language "make it darker / slower / more mineral," or scene suggestions. Big feature, but interesting given you already have the Generate/theme system.
What you already do that they don't
OSC/OSCQuery two-way control, the audiovisual-relations theory layer (coupling, Worlds, temperament, Basanta/Chion lineage), the vision-bus return path, real-time GLSL datamosh + the native-node engine, monocular depth/2.5D, the Cameraless and Corbeil-Perron homages, and desktop-native output. Their datamosh is offline codec (ffmosh/JPEGGED); yours is real-time. You're a much deeper instrument — they're a wider, friendlier canvas.

My pick of the four most worth building, in order: X/Y pad → Setlist → per-layer masking → quick audio-bind. Want me to build any of them? The X/Y pad is the highest value-to-effort and slots straight into your Meta layer.
