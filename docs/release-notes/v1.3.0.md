**Palinopsia v1.3.0 : the audio update.**

Palinopsia listens to more than a microphone now, and plays along with others. A new
Audio Layer plays sound files, live inputs and NDI® sources beside Sonify, a film's own soundtrack or
any sound file can drive the picture, Ableton Link keeps the picture on the music's
beat, and a new Assemblage voice sings your edits. Persistent controls, effects, sources
and modulators hold through every scene change, the Mixer becomes the home of A and B,
the layer strip is a third shorter, modulations can be shaped, Ctrl+K finds anything,
and you can write your own shaders, or let an AI agent play along. Two clouds painted in
oil, four new Lattice forms, a real codec datamosh and four effects that keep the
picture in a store join the sources and effects. From this version Palinopsia is free
software, under the GPL.

## Fixed before release

Everything new in this version went through two full audits before release. Among the
fixes you may notice :

- **Coupling behaves.** A cut on a held note no longer parks the layer on B, the gate's
  tightness hardens its edge as it says (and no longer flickers between A and B when the
  sound hovers on its threshold), the band feature listens to the band you pick, and a
  recalled scene no longer snaps a drifting layer's mix back. With a harmony set, B no
  longer flickers when its hue sits opposite the target.
- **The dome's alignment grid no longer comes back on its own.** It starts off each time
  the app opens, opening a session never turns it on, and turning the dome off puts it
  away, so it can't end up burned into a show.
- **A session never turns a microphone on.** A live input saved in a session comes back
  closed, with an **open** button; picking an input opens it, an installation opens its
  inputs at boot. An unplugged input says **lost** and comes back with its device, and one
  that fails to open while its device is there tries again by itself. Sonify's Filter
  **line-in** is the same : a session, a scene, a preset or an undo never opens it, and
  one saved on waits for one click on its button (a dim ring until then).
- **The BPM box** changes the tempo when you press Enter or leave the box, and Escape puts
  the old one back : the tempo no longer dips to 20 while you type, and Live and the
  others never hear a half-typed one. A typed tempo is one undo step, undo works while
  linked (even while a linked app ramps the tempo), and peers coming and going no longer
  ask "Save changes?". **Escape in any number box** now cancels the edit, and so does
  leaving a box you emptied.
- **Ableton Link holds.** With other apps linked, the modulators, the grain sync and Sonify
  stay on the shared tempo while the scene pad blends scenes saved at other tempos, and
  the **MIDI clock out** follows Link's beat, its Start waiting for the next Link bar, so
  your gear's bars line up with every linked app's. The Sonify sequencers no longer stall
  when a linked app re-counts its bars as it starts playing.
- **The Audio Layer** : a film dropped on a slot plays its soundtrack, a file with loop
  off stops at its out point, New and Generate fade the layer out over the Morph time
  (an undo during the fade brings it back), undo covers it (never opening an input), and
  a file slot's volume goes to 200 %. Switching Sonify off while it plays no longer clicks.
- **Persistent holds against everything** : the Meta dice, an effect's own dice, the
  Finishing dice and reset, Init background, the World amount and the World page leave a
  persistent control alone, and the rack dice and Randomize keep persistent effects in
  place and deal the others around them. The pos X/Y sliders, the small toggles and the
  Vibe palette's color stops can be made persistent too. Swapping a source by hand
  unpins that slot's controls.
- **The World amount** fades only what the World changed : below 1 your sliders and
  modulators keep working, and 0 goes back to the mood you had. Switching Worlds no longer
  fades the layers out and back in. A broken World can no longer crash the app.
- **Morphs keep the sound.** A layer playing the same sound in both scenes plays on
  through the morph, and other sounds fade with their pictures instead of being cut. A
  FLOW or STEP binding keeps its place through a scene change.
- **New and Vary on an Assemblage** keep its effects, framing, speed and modulation, and
  an edit cut from the Inspector can be saved and exported.
- **The Source Editor** no longer saves over the shader you were on when you open another
  one, and Ctrl+Z no longer brings that one's code back; deleting a shader that plays
  keeps it playing. Saving code with errors keeps the last good version playing, the
  preview survives a GPU reset, the editor tells you when your shader was changed
  elsewhere (reload / keep mine), and every version written over or deleted is kept in the
  Shaders folder's `.history`. A whole-number control with a wide range is a slider (a long
  menu could freeze the app). On a Mac it opens with **Cmd+Option+E**, so Option+E types
  accents again.
- **The agent** changes only what it is asked (an effect's other controls stay where
  they are), its undo takes back only its own changes, never yours, it never writes over
  one of your shaders unless told to replace it, and turning it off closes every
  connection.
- **The Output.** Right-click the Output button : **Watch the Output in a window** (it was
  "Open Output in a window") no longer pulls a fullscreen or spanned output off the
  projector; it says why instead. The Output page in its own window keeps Esc, Enter, the
  arrows and Delete to itself, stays live while the instrument is minimized, and its edits
  count as changes (leaving asks to save).
- **Clearer panels.** Right-click menus are fully opaque and on top, even on a grayed-out
  control; a knob dragged to zero no longer sticks to the mouse; grabbing a modulated
  slider no longer makes it jump. Number boxes always fit their numbers, the layer's OUT
  tabs size to their words (`normal · no mask · fb 0.85`, the feedback amount never cut),
  and the Mixer's coupling takes two lines, the mode then the feature, so "transient" and
  "centroid" read in full.
- **Session media** relinks only what is missing and never confuses two files of the same
  name or size; copies made on another computer can be made again.
- **The fulldome panorama's seam** stays behind the audience whatever the rotation, and
  Space follows the fill offset exactly. The offset sliders have no dead travel, and a
  film covers the dome in the Inspector too (Reset clears its fit).
- **Dropping files** : a file dropped on the output window no longer replaces the
  show, and a session file opens wherever it is dropped.
- **Oil Cloud and Cloud Study are picked by hand** : Randomize and the Background dice no
  longer deal them. On the dome, Oil Cloud is traced at full height again (a wide frame was
  cut to about half, soft vertically), and both cost less for the same picture. Lattice's
  tunnel shows its far end again, and its galaxy and swell no longer jump after a long run.
  The Oil on Canvas, Warm and Cool, Codec Rot and Keyframe Hunger chains keep the scene's
  palette mix.
- **The gate hair is gone** (the Finalizer's hair stuck on an edge of the frame) : its
  slider and the hair itself. Dust, scratches, the gauge, dirt and burst are as they were.
- Codec Mosh no longer restarts, freezes or flashes an old frame; the Assemblage voice
  follows key glides and its cut accents ring on the new note; the Ring bank's top
  octave no longer whistles; a sound analysis cut short is made again at the next launch.

## New

- **Search everything.** Press **Ctrl+K** (Cmd+K on a Mac) and type : a palette finds any
  effect, source, preset, master chain, Generate theme, page or command, and every
  parameter of what is loaded. **Enter** picks : a source goes in the selected slot, an
  effect in the selected rack, a preset onto the unit playing its shader, and a
  parameter is shown in the Inspector and flashed so you can find it. Every pick is one
  Ctrl+Z, **Esc** closes it, several words narrow the search (`mosh l2`), and with the
  field empty your recent picks come first.

- **Field Store**, a new node : a coarse copy of the picture that never fades, written
  only where a key lets it through (the bright parts, the edges, what moves, another
  layer, or the layer's own shape). A moving figure leaves hard stamps of itself instead
  of a soft trail. Eight modes : trail, color trail, ghost, catch-up, mosaic, **reveal**
  (the picture painted in by what moves), **protect** (a figure vanishes into what was
  there before) and **acquire** (the first arrivals kept). It captures every frame,
  every few frames, in bursts, on the beat or by hand, from 64 to 512 cells across and 2
  to 8 bits deep; its stamps can slide away in lattices, and it can hand its store to the
  next scene. Ten presets, three master chains (Hard Memory, Sixteen Levels, Strobe
  Ghost) and two Generate themes (Field Store, Revealed).

- **Shatter, Crawl and Store Zoom**, three new effects. **Shatter** shows motion as
  texture : a still is grabbed again and again (at a rate, on the beat or on grab ▸) and
  a pattern (checker, lines, bricks, dither, cells, the picture's own darks…) switches
  between it and the live picture, so still areas look untouched and anything that
  moves breaks into the pattern, without ever flashing as a whole. **Crawl** colours
  each place by how long ago it last changed : motion leaves bands of colour behind it,
  and the palette turns slowly through them so the contours crawl. **Store Zoom** is a
  hardware frame store's zoom : one zoom that enlarges and cuts into uneven blocks at
  once (or only mosaics in place), tall or wide pixels, a scroll that wraps around the
  frame and a mirror that moves with it. Eight presets each, a new **Frame Store** group
  of master chains, and three Generate themes (Shatter, Trail Crawl, Store Zoom).

- **Clearer families in the pickers.** The effects picker has new **Frame Store**,
  **Analog**, **Codec**, **Optical** and **Film** groups, so Glitch is half its old size
  and no group holds a single effect; the source pickers gain **Painted** (Oil Cloud,
  Cloud Study) and **Geometry** sections, and Silhouette its 👤. In Generate, the store
  looks are one **Frame Store** family.

- **The Feel macros are saved with each session.** Density, Gesture ⇄ Texture, Coalesce,
  Tonicity, Drift and Flow come back with the session they belong to; New sets them
  neutral, and a Generate's Feel no longer follows you into every session after (a
  Density left low by a theme quietly faded the upper layers everywhere).

- **50 add**, a new blend mode just before add : fade a layer in and it is added on top of
  what is below at the middle, then the layers below fade out under it, the way a mixer
  fades clips. On the A/B mix, the same from A to B.

- **Persistent.** Right-click any control, or a whole layer, and choose **Persistent** :
  from then on no scene, Randomize, Variation, Generate, World or preset changes it,
  while everything around it moves. Its modulation stays with it, and it never wavers in
  a crossfade. A thin gold bar marks a persistent control, a gold border a persistent
  layer, and the scene bank lists them all. The same menu has **Set in every scene** :
  what plays now goes into every scene at once (undo brings them back).
  Whole effects, sources and modulators hold too. Right-click an effect for
  **Persistent effect** : it stays in its rack at its place, settings and modulation and
  all. Right-click a source card A or B for **Persistent source** : the slot keeps its
  source (a film with its framing and transport) and its effects; swap the source by
  hand and the slot keeps the new one. Right-click a modulator card for **Persistent
  modulator** : its settings stay, while what it drives follows each scene (except
  persistent controls, which keep their modulator). A gold border or outline marks each.

- **A clearer, smaller layer strip.** Each layer now reads top to bottom in the order the
  picture goes through it, its four steps named in the margin (IN, MIX, FX, OUT), each
  on a soft panel of its own color : its two sources side by side, the mix where they
  meet, the layer's effects, and how it lands on the layers below (blend, mask and
  feedback as tabs that show where each stands and open on a click). Speed and Vary share the last line, and FB sits in the layer header again,
  beside S and ⊘. A typical layer is about a third shorter. An effect's move arrows
  show when you hover it, so two effects share a line. A shape mask's place is now an
  X/Y pad. Each source's effects, and the layer's, have a power button that turns the
  whole chain off and on, and the A/B mix shows in percent, ready to type.
- **The Mixer, the home of A and B.** Each layer's column now shows how its two sources
  meet: the mode and an A–B crossfader with what it means in that mode, a white line where
  the sound pushes it, the **hue** harmony with swatches of the colors, and the
  **coupling** to the sound with a live meter and two knobs, above opacity, speed and
  blend, and an **active** / **inactive** word that says whether the layer shows at all (and
  why not). A new **≈ coupling** switch pauses every layer's coupling at once. Saved mixes
  now keep all of it. The layer strips get lighter: their MIX row says what its slider
  means, and a ≈ chip shows when the sound is moving it.
  The coupling now **matches the sound's level** (**auto**, in each column's coupling,
  on by default) : a quiet track moves the mix as far as a loud one, a loud one swings
  instead of sitting at the top, and silence or room noise leaves it still; the meter
  shows what it hears. A transient fires on the hits of whatever plays, not only the
  loud ones, so a World's cut on transient flashes more on a busy track. Click **auto**
  off for the raw response, as before.
- **Harmony means something now.** It sets B's colors against A's: to the left B takes
  A's colors, to the right their opposite, and the middle leaves B alone. The weave
  mix follows it too. It can be modulated, MIDI-learned and played over OSC.
- **Oil Cloud, a new source** : one billowing cloud floating in the dark, ray-traced and
  painted in oil, gold toward the light and blue in its folds, laid down in strokes that
  follow its forms. Grow, density, a second density for the small puffs, rotation, zoom
  and light shape it, swirl curls it like stirred paint, and it swells with the bass. Its
  ground can be transparent, to float it over your other layers. Crisp on the dome too.
  Ten presets, a Warm and Cool master chain and an Oil Cloud Generate theme.
- **Cloud Study, a new source** : a sky of billowing cloud, ray-traced and lit by a low sun,
  then painted over in thick oil, the strokes following the forms, the paint catching
  the light, the canvas showing through the thin sky. The clouds drift and churn; the
  view runs from a wall of cloud overhead to a deck reaching the horizon. Nine presets,
  from Golden hour to Storm front and Nocturne, an Oil on Canvas master chain and an
  Oil Sky Generate theme.
- **Lattice has four more forms** : a **tunnel** of dots to fly down forever (a warp
  tunnel with streak), and a **sphere**, a **torus** and a spiral **galaxy** the camera
  circles, which fly spins, rolls and wheels. On the full dome the Lattice keeps its
  proportions (a sphere stays round) and the shapes sit in the middle of the dome. With
  ten presets and two Generate themes, Warp Tunnel and Orbit. Perspective also keeps its
  proportions on the dome.
- **Right-click the Output button** to **Watch the Output in a window** beside the controls
  (the domemaster on the dome), and tweak while you watch it, or to open **the Output
  page in its own window** : the whole page beside the instrument or on another screen,
  every control live while you play; your keys still play the instrument from there. ⇲
  back into the app puts it back. Otherwise the page opens in the app, as always.
- **The modulator types are sorted into families** in their menu : waves, rhythms, random,
  gestures, and listening (the sound, the picture, your body).
- **Shape a modulation.** Each binding has a new ⌇ button opening three tools between the
  modulator and the parameter : **combine** it with one or two other modulators (bass ×
  LFO swings only with the bass, LFO − kick ducks on the kick, max lets either one drive
  it), a **threshold** so only real hits move the picture, and **integrate**, which makes
  each hit push the value forward and keeps it there : the kick turns a rotation, a hue or
  a camera one notch per beat, and the picture stays where the music left it.
- **Let an AI agent play along.** Palinopsia can now be an MCP server : turn on
  **Agent (MCP)** in the Connect tab and an agent on your computer (Claude Code, for
  one) can read what is playing, look at the output, set sources, controls, effects and
  scenes, drive anything that has an OSC address, and write new shaders, seeing each
  one run before handing it to you. Every change it makes is one Ctrl+Z, your Inspector
  stays where you left it, it cannot touch the Flash safety, and only your own computer
  can connect. Off until you turn it on.
- **Solid Color greys out the controls it is not using** (the gradient's, or the flat
  color's).
- **Write your own shaders.** The new **Source Editor** (`Alt+E`, `Cmd+Option+E` on a Mac,
  or `{ }` in the bottom bar) is a page for writing generators and effects : start from a template, from a
  copy of any built-in one, or from an ISF file found anywhere. It compiles as you type
  into a live preview (the last version that worked keeps playing, and a mistake is
  marked on its line), with the shader's controls right there to try it. Save it and it
  is one of yours, in the source and effect menus like any built-in, saved in a Shaders
  folder; save again while it plays and it changes live. A control's `"HINT"` in the
  header is its hover help. Sessions carry the shaders they play, so they open the same
  on another computer.
- **Sonify hears your edits.** A new **Assemblage** voice plays an Assemblage's own
  sound, cut with the picture : every cut is its clip's own sound from its in point, the
  clip's place on the Assemble map gives its note and pan (look-alike clips sing alike),
  a strike of the resonators marks each cut, and slowed clips sound slowed, like tape (or
  keep their pitch). The Collage's filters, compressor and Ring bank are there too. The
  Collage and Assemblage voices grey out while there is nothing for them to hear. (In
  Space, the FX return and the Collage pieces each move up one source.)
- **A better World page.** It says what applying a World will change, morphs into it,
  lets you pick which layers it couples, edits its film character and more of the
  Context mood, reads a World from what is on screen, previews on your own layers and
  your live sound, marks built-ins you changed (save as mine, reset), steps through
  Worlds with the arrow keys, and never takes a modulator slot that is yours. And a
  **World amount** dial, playable by modulators, Meta knobs and OSC, fades the World's
  mood in and out live.
- **Codec Mosh, the real datamosh.** A new effect sends the picture through an actual
  video encoder and decoder and withholds the keyframes between them, the way glitch
  artists break video files : at a cut, the new motion drags the old picture around
  until a clean frame returns. Repeat or drop motion frames for more damage, starve the
  quality into blocks, or let another layer's movement push this one around. With
  presets, two master chains (Codec Rot, Keyframe Hunger) and a Generate theme.

- **Ableton Link.** Palinopsia now plays in time with Live, DJ software and every
  Link app on your network : enable it in the Connect tab and the tempo is shared
  (anyone changes it, everyone follows), and the bar too : modulators synced to the
  BPM and the Sonify sequencers land on the same downbeat as the music. A latency
  setting keeps the picture on the beat through a slow projector. Turn on **start / stop
  sync** (and Start Stop Sync in Live's Link/Tempo/MIDI preferences) and pressing play in
  Live starts Palinopsia on the bar : the MIDI clock's Start (the gear it drives starts
  with Live), the scene sequencer if the session uses it, and the Sonify
  sequencers; stop stops them. A **PLAYING / STOPPED** button plays or stops every linked
  app, and starting a sequencer or the MIDI clock here plays everyone. The Sonify
  sequencers join in once they have run : on the very first play they wait for you, after
  that a Link stop stops them and the next play brings them back. Turned on while Live
  already plays, it can take a few seconds to catch the play.

- **The Audio Layer.** A new strip under Layer 4 in the layers tab, for the sound : two
  slots, A and B, each a sound file, a live audio input (a microphone, your interface's
  inputs, one channel or a stereo pair) or the sound of an **NDI® source** on the
  network, each with its own volume, pan and mute, a mix
  between them and the layer's own volume, with meters. A file has the Import audio
  transport and waveform. It plays through Sonify's output (the EQ, the limiter, the
  recordings and Space have it), Sonify on or off, so a live input or a soundtrack blends
  with Sonify's voices. Saved with the session, and on OSC (`/opsia/audiolayer/…`). A
  modulator can listen to its files too.
- **NDI® sound in.** An Audio Layer slot can play another computer's sound over the
  network : a DAW's NDI audio plug-in, NDI Tools, OBS, picked from the same list as the
  NDI video input. A stereo pair or one channel, into the layer's mix, Sonify's output,
  the recordings and Space. It takes a few seconds to connect, then plays on a steady
  buffer of about 50 ms (the Inspector shows it); it says **lost** when the source leaves
  the network and plays again when it is back. Like a live input, a session never opens
  it by itself.
- **The Audio Layer can be modulated.** Its volume and mix, each slot's volume and pan,
  and a sound file's speed, in and out and playhead have **M** chips like any control. A
  modulated playhead scrubs : a sample and hold cuts, a slow sweep grains, a fast one
  scratches, a held value just plays. Volumes glide (no clicks), mute still wins, and the
  Meta knobs reach them too.
- **Sound drives the picture from a film, or from any sound file.** An audio modulator
  can now listen to a layer's clip instead of the audio input : the soundtrack of a film,
  or a sound file, read exactly where its playhead is. Reversed, scrubbed, at any speed
  or in grains, the modulator follows, even though the film plays silent. A stopped clip
  is silence.
- **Import audio.** A new source plays a sound file in a layer like a film (play, loop,
  in and out points, speed, a playhead you can scrub or modulate) and shows its waveform :
  the whole file, the loop or a window scrolling past the playhead, filled, outlined or as
  bars, the part already played lit. It plays on Sonify's output device (for a sound in
  the recordings and in Space, put it on the Audio Layer). Presets, and three master
  chains made for it (Sound : Phosphor, Tape Floor, Relief).
- **A film's sound, seen and heard.** The video transport has a small level meter : the
  clip's sound where the playhead is, with its RMS as a number, so you see whether there is
  sound right there. And a volume : off by default, as films always were, turned up the
  film plays its own sound (forward, at its own pace; not in the recordings).

- **Output EQ presets** : save the whole EQ under a name (a room, a venue, headphones)
  and recall it from a little menu on the EQ, or over OSC. A preset keeps the glue too
  (on or off, its amount, its voice); flat leaves the glue as it plays.
- **Sonify's Filter voice can play the Audio Layer** : pick **AU** as its source and the
  layer's sound goes through the image's filters. Mute the layer's own strip to hear it
  only that way.
- **The output EQ and the glue are now in the audio tab** (Audio section), since they
  shape everything that leaves, Sonify and the Audio Layer; the Audio Layer's volume is
  there too, beside Sonify's. The Audio Layer stays out of Sonify's delay and reverb.

- **The Audio Layer can be the audio tab's input.** Pick "Audio Layer" in the input menu
  and everything that reacts to sound (the coupling, the audio modulators) reacts to
  what the layer plays, a sound file or your interface's input, without opening the
  microphone.

- **Play both files together.** A play button between the Audio Layer's A and B starts
  both sound files from their in points at the same moment, in sync; again pauses both.
  And a modulator can now listen to the Audio Layer's whole sound (`AU all` : both slots
  mixed, a live input too).

- **The audio section shows when sound is going on** : its dot lights while Sonify or the
  Audio Layer plays, and says which.

- **Ignore global speed** : a toggle in the transport so a clip, a sound above all,
  plays at its own speed while the whole composition's speed changes.

## Changed

- **On the full dome, things turn and fall the dome's way** (fill) : Transform's rotate
  turns around the zenith, Feedback Zoom and the Feedback node nest their echoes in
  circles, and Ash, Optical Rain, Erosion, Streak, Light Trails, Murmuration's wind,
  Sync Loss and Tracking fall or flow outward from the zenith, down the walls. No frame
  corner swings into view, and at the front everything reads as it does flat. Flat
  output is unchanged.
- **Flash safety is tighter on the dome** : left at mild (its default), it goes to strong
  while the Fulldome output is on, since a flash fills the whole view on a dome, and back
  to mild after; a level you set yourself stays.
- **The Ring bank opens all the way.** Its cutoff (Collage and Assemblage) now reaches an
  octave above the bank's highest band, and the voices' range goes up to octave 9, so the
  bank can be fully open and bright.
- **Assemblage… in a layer's source menu plays right there** : your latest edit lands
  on the slot and the Inspector shows how it plays (which edit, new, vary, the
  transport), with a button to the assemble tab when you want it.
- **The side tabs are reordered** : layers, mixer, finishing, feel, connect,
  performance, assemble.
- **Palinopsia is now free software under the GPL** (version 3 or later), so that
  Ableton Link can be built in. Versions up to 1.2.10 stay MIT.
- **The audio/midi/osc tab is now called Connect** (still key A).
- **Drag files in from your desktop** : a film, a sound or a folder of films onto a
  layer, a sound onto the Audio Layer, a session file onto the window.
- **Recent sessions** under Open ▾.
- **Missing media, found again.** When a session opens and some of its films or sounds
  are gone (another computer, a renamed drive), a banner names them : **Relink…** finds
  them by name in a folder you pick. **Collect into the session's folder** copies every
  file a session uses next to it, so the session travels between computers in one piece.
  Open ▾ → Session media… lists them all.
- **A bigger picture when you want it** : drag the bar under the preview to size it,
  or press **V** for a big preview; the preview no longer gets squeezed to nothing by a
  tall Inspector.
- **The session buttons sit together** in the top bar (the list, Load, New, Open, Save,
  Save As).

- **Vibe Palette and Context, clearer** : each section's header says what it is doing
  (even folded), controls that do nothing right now are grayed with the reason (never
  while a modulator moves them), color rows are just their swatch and value, and the
  Vibe's palette is a single strip showing exactly the colors your picture goes through,
  a chord's included : click a stop to recolor it.
- **Stutter** lost its fire button.

- **A tidier Master FX strip** : its controls on one line (the chain presets and + fx menus
  the same size), the effects underneath across the whole width.
- **Meta knobs show their value** : a live number under each knob, what it sends now
  through its curve, as you turn it, from MIDI or from a modulator.

## Fixed

- **Better domes.** The Satosphère button now also makes the composition square while
  the dome is on (your own size comes back when it goes off), so round things stay round
  on the dome (a 16:9 frame stretched them front to back). Context's depth and edge
  darkening stay round and keep the walls lit, CRT Screen no longer draws a black ring
  around the rim, films fill the dome, and the panorama's seam behind the audience
  blends instead of cutting. The Finalizer's film and digital grain is fine and round on
  the dome again (it came out blown up and oblong), and fine patterns no longer shimmer
  where the dome squeezes the picture.
- **The ADSR modulator's times are no longer cut off** beside their sliders.
- **The dome stays perfectly round when you move the picture.** In full-dome fill, an
  offset used to cut the dome into a pointed lens; now the picture's center moves and
  the whole dome stays covered.
- **The audio input could stay silent** when the device chosen in the audio tab was no
  longer found (unplugged, or numbered differently by another version of the app) : the
  audio modulators then read nothing, even from a laptop's microphone. It now finds the
  device again by its name, or plays the default input and tells you so. Choosing
  another device while the input runs now switches to it right away.

- **Import audio plays AIFF files** (and WMA, CAF) : they were refused.

## Opening an older session

Everything opens as before : the Audio Layer is empty, films stay silent (their volume
at 0), nothing is persistent, every modulation goes straight to its parameter (no
shaping), and the Lattice keeps its form. A few things look or sound different on
purpose :

- **The Feel macros** (Density and the others) open neutral : older sessions didn't carry
  them. Set them again and save once.
- **Harmony** now sets B's colors against A's : a layer saved with a harmony above 0
  pushes B toward A's opposite colors. At 0, nothing changes.
- **On the full dome** (fill), a session that turns with Transform, Feedback Zoom or the
  Feedback node, or uses Ash, Optical Rain, Erosion, Streak, Light Trails, Murmuration,
  Sync Loss or Tracking, now turns and falls the dome's way. Flat, nothing changes.
- **The coupling** opens level-matched (**auto** on) : a coupled layer moves further on
  quiet material than it did (a lean on flux swings fully instead of a percent or two),
  and a cut on transient fires on more hits. Click **auto** off in the Mixer for the
  response it had.
- **The Ring bank's cutoff** (Collage and Assemblage) reaches further : a saved cutoff
  below the top sits a few semitones higher.
- **Stutter's fire button** is gone : a value saved for it is ignored, and a binding to it
  is cleaned up.
- **The gate hair** is gone : a scene or session that had it opens without it, along with
  any modulation, Meta knob or persistent pin aimed at it.
- **Impasto** : a session or preset saved with it opens with Cloud Study.
- **The dome's alignment grid** is never restored by a session : it stays as this computer
  has it (off at launch), and a save writes it off.
- **A session saved without a tempo** opens at 120 BPM.
- **Sonify's Filter line-in**, saved on, comes back closed : one click on its **line-in**
  button opens it (an installation opens it at boot).
- **In Space**, the FX return and the Collage pieces each move up one source (the
  Assemblage voice takes the place before them) : a SpatGRIS setup that expects them
  where they were needs renumbering.
- **Mixer presets** saved before set what they always held; the A/B mix, mode, harmony
  and coupling, which they did not hold, stay as they are.
- A World whose sound modulator sat on M8 in an older session is still recognized as
  the World's.

Builds are unsigned :

  (run from the disk image or Downloads, macOS runs a hidden temporary copy), then run
  `xattr -cr /Applications/Palinopsia.app` once in Terminal, or open it and click **Open
  Anyway** in System Settings → Privacy & Security. The first launch asks for the local
  network, and for the camera and the microphone when you use them : accept, or OSC, NDI,
  Ableton Link and light output stay silent. Screen capture is the one permission macOS
  never prompts for : switch Palinopsia on under Privacy & Security → Screen & System
  Audio Recording, then start it again.

**Intel Macs (x64)** : this release ships Apple Silicon only. On an Intel Mac, build from
source (`npm install`, then `npm run build:mac`).

## For developers

- **Persistent** (`persist.ts`) : a pin is a key (a control's `modTargetKey`,
  `lay:<i>:speed`, `layer:<i>`), held in the store and saved with the session (`pins`).
  Every bulk write (a scene, Randomize, Generate, a World, a preset) goes through one
  gate, `keepPersistent(incoming, live, pins)`, before its morph starts; a new bulk
  write must call it too.
- **Persistent, whole** : `fxunit:<rack>:<id>` (an effect), `src:<i>:<A|B>` (a source
  slot) and `mod:<n>` (a modulator card) go through the same gate; a whole pin wins over
  the control pins inside it.
- **Shared GLSL at an include line** : `// @dome` pastes `lib/dome.glsl` (rotations and
  "down" on the dome's disc under full-dome fill; a shader that turns or drops things
  should use it) and `// @grain` the Finalizer's grain (`lib/finalGrain.glsl`, which the
  dome stage also draws after the mapping). `shaders/isf/index.ts` does it for every
  shader; the native Feedback node takes `dome.glsl` too.
- **The tempo for ISF shaders** : `engine/tempoSignal.ts`, read by a phase's integrand in
  `phases.ts` (Shatter's beat-synced grabs); it counts on the rack's own clock, like
  Decimate.
- **Field Store** : `engine/fieldStore.ts`, a native node; its store can be parked across
  a scene change (`keep`), and `Compositor.panic` empties the parking.
- **Search everything** : `searchIndex.ts` builds what Ctrl+K finds from the registries
  and the composition each time it opens; a control is found by the `data-ctl` on its
  `PinZone`.
- **Shaping a binding** (`engine/modShape.ts`) : `ModAssignment.shape` (combine,
  threshold, integrate) runs between the modulator and depth; `sanitizeModShape` on
  load.
- **Your own shaders** : ids are `user-<slug>` (`shared/userShaderId.ts`, shared by main
  and page), files in `Shaders/` (`main/userShaders.ts`), registered by
  `shaders/userShaders.ts`; sessions carry the ones they play (`Session.userShaders`).
  Lint, compile and stills live in `shaders/shaderCheck.ts`, used by the Source Editor
  and the agent alike.
- **Agent (MCP)** : `main/mcp.ts` (Streamable HTTP on 127.0.0.1, off by default), the
  tool list in `shared/mcpTools.ts`, run by `renderer/src/mcp/tools.ts`; every write is
  one undo step and never reaches `strobeSafe`.
- **Ableton Link** : the Link SDK compiled into a small N-API addon (`native/link`),
  built by CI on all three platforms; `main/link.ts` polls it, `renderer/src/link.ts`
  extrapolates the beat. A deliberate tempo edit goes through `tempoEvents.ts`. Start /
  stop sync : the addon's `startStopSync` / `setPlaying` and `state().playing`; a clocked
  thing started or stopped on purpose calls `playEdited` (`playEvents.ts`), and a new one
  must too.
- **Coupling level-matching** : `engine/couplingMatch.ts` (a peak and floor follower per
  layer, a shared silence gate), off per layer with `LayerCoupling.auto === false`.
- **The Audio Layer under modulation** : `ModTarget` kind `'au'` (`shared/auMod.ts`),
  written per frame by the modulation pass into `engine/audioLayerMod.ts` and applied by
  the audio engine, never through the store. **NDI sound in** : one audio-only receiver
  per slot in the preload (`preload/ndi.ts`), its samples sent over a `MessageChannel`
  straight to the slot's worklet (`audio/ndiAudio.worklet.js`, the jitter buffer).
- **Round on the dome** : `DOME_FILL` (`engine/domeSignal.ts`) is a plain uniform any
  shader can read (the horizon's radius under fisheye + fill, else 0), and a pass size
  can read `$DOME_FILL` too (Oil Cloud traces at full size on the dome that way).
- **A/B harmony** : `engine/abHarmony.ts` (`HARMONY_GLSL` on the GPU, `harmonizeRgb` for
  the Mixer's swatch), the sources' colors from a small means pass read back only while
  needed.

- `main/audioAnalysis.ts` : a sound's analysis, once, cached (a copy of `audioIn.ts`'s
  AnalyserNode maths at 60 frames a second + the peaks); `engine/clipAudio.ts` reads it at
  a slot's playhead (`videoPlayheads` + the slot's original file). Keep the two analysers
  identical : a modulator must react to a clip as it would to the same sound played live.
- `audio/audioLayer.ts` : the Audio Layer's graph (no store import : App syncs it).
  It joins Sonify's worklet as input 2 (`setExternalInput`); `setLayerWanted` keeps the
  engine running with Sonify off (voices masked in `pushConfig`); `onContext` rebuilds it
  on a healed context. `engine/clipPlayer.ts` : the sound-file transport shared with
  `AudioSource`. Its slots key their playheads under layer -1 (`AUDIO_LAYER`).
- `engine/mediaSink.ts` : every layer `<audio>` / `<video>` that may sound follows
  Sonify's output device (`trackMediaSink`).
