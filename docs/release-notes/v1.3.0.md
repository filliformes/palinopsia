**Palinopsia v1.3.0 : the audio update.** (in progress)

## New

- **The Audio Layer.** A new strip under Layer 4 in the layers tab, for the sound : two
  slots, A and B, each a sound file or a live audio input (a microphone, your interface's
  inputs, one channel or a stereo pair), each with its own volume, pan and mute, a mix
  between them and the layer's own volume, with meters. A file has the Import audio
  transport and waveform. It plays through Sonify's output (the EQ, the limiter, the
  recordings and Space have it), Sonify on or off, so a live input or a soundtrack blends
  with Sonify's voices. Saved with the session, and on OSC (`/opsia/audiolayer/…`). A
  modulator can listen to its files too.
- **Sound drives the picture from a film, or from any sound file.** An audio modulator
  can now listen to a layer's clip instead of the audio input : the soundtrack of a film,
  or a sound file, read exactly where its playhead is. Reversed, scrubbed, at any speed
  or in grains, the modulator follows, even though the film plays silent. A stopped clip
  is silence.
- **Import audio.** A new source plays a sound file in a layer like a film (play, loop,
  in and out points, speed, a playhead you can scrub or modulate) and shows its waveform :
  the whole file, the loop or a window scrolling past the playhead, filled, outlined or as
  bars, the part already played lit. It plays on Sonify's output device. Presets, and
  three master chains made for it (Sound : Phosphor, Tape Floor, Relief).
- **A film's sound, seen and heard.** The video transport has a small level meter : the
  clip's sound where the playhead is, with its RMS as a number, so you see whether there is
  sound right there. And a volume : off by default, as films always were, turned up the
  film plays its own sound (forward, at its own pace).

- **The audio section shows when sound is going on** : its dot lights while Sonify or the
  Audio Layer plays, and says which.

- **Ignore global speed** : a toggle in the transport so a clip, a sound above all,
  plays at its own speed while the whole composition's speed changes.

## Fixed

- **The audio input could stay silent** when the device chosen in the audio tab was no
  longer found (unplugged, or numbered differently by another version of the app) : the
  audio modulators then read nothing, even from a laptop's microphone. It now finds the
  device again by its name, or plays the default input and tells you so. Choosing
  another device while the input runs now switches to it right away.

- **Import audio plays AIFF files** (and WMA, CAF) : they were refused.

## For developers

- `main/audioAnalysis.ts` : a sound's analysis, once, cached (a copy of `audioIn.ts`'s
  AnalyserNode maths at 60 frames a second + the peaks); `engine/clipAudio.ts` reads it at
  a slot's playhead (`videoPlayheads` + the slot's original file). Keep the two analysers
  identical : a modulator must react to a clip as it would to the same sound played live.
- `audio/audioLayer.ts` : the Audio Layer's graph (no store import : App syncs it).
  It joins Sonify's worklet as input 2 (`setExternalInput`); `setLayerWanted` keeps the
  engine running with Sonify off (voices masked in `pushConfig`); `onContext` rebuilds it
  on a healed context. `engine/clipPlayer.ts` : the sound-file transport shared with
  `AudioSource`. Its slots key their playheads under layer -1 (`AUDIO_LAYER`).
- `engine/mediaSink.ts` : every layer `<audio>` / `<video>` that may sound follows
  Sonify's output device (`trackMediaSink`).
