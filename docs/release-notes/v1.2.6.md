**Palinopsia v1.2.6**

## Highlights

- **Perspective**, a new effect. It lays a layer on a plane in space and looks at it
  through a lens : lean it back into a floor running to the horizon, swing it into a
  wall that runs off to the side, curl it like a curved screen or ripple it into a
  travelling ribbon. **lens** goes from a flat telephoto to a wide angle, **fog** lets the
  far side melt into the layers below, **travel** slides the picture along the plane. Put
  it after Lowercase, Op-Art or Interference and flat line fields become architecture.

- **Lowercase : slant**, a ninth figure. Full-height bars that each lean their own way and
  cross into X shapes and fans, thin ones and fat ones, dealt on the step and held like
  every other figure. **split** is how far they lean, **density** how many stand. Three
  presets : Crossing, Leaning comb, Fallen bars.
