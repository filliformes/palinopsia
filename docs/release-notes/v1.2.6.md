**Palinopsia v1.2.6 : points and planes.**

A release about space. **Lattice** puts points in three dimensions in front of a moving
camera, **Perspective** lays any layer on a plane that runs off to the horizon, and
Lowercase learns to lean its bars into crossing slants. A new **Space** family in Generate
and a **Space** group of master chains reach all of it in one click. The fulldome output
becomes something you can play : every mapping slider takes a modulator or a Meta knob,
spin reaches a full turn a second, and feather softens the picture's own edges. And the
console, which Chromium had been filling with a readback warning thirteen times a second
since launch, is quiet.

## Highlights

- **Lattice**, a new source : a lattice of points in space seen through a moving camera.
  A floor of dots that sinks into a gravity well and rings with ripples, heaves like a
  sea or rises with the sound or with another layer's picture; or a room full of points
  the camera flies through forever, streaking them into a warp. Overlapping dots never
  pile up into a glow, and the ground can be transparent so the layers below show
  between the points. Ten presets, from Gravity well and Calm sea to Crystal room and
  Warp.

- **Perspective**, a new effect. It lays a layer on a plane in space and looks at it
  through a lens : lean it back into a floor running to the horizon, swing it into a
  wall that runs off to the side, curl it like a curved screen or ripple it into a
  travelling ribbon. **lens** goes from a flat telephoto to a wide angle, **fog** lets the
  far side melt into the layers below, **travel** slides the picture along the plane. Put
  it after Lowercase, Op-Art or Interference and flat line fields become architecture.

- **Lowercase : slant**, a ninth figure. Full-height bars that each lean their own way and
  cross into X shapes and fans, thin ones and fat ones, dealt on the step and held like
  every other figure. **split** is how far they lean, **density** how many stand. Three
  presets : Crossing, Leaning comb, Fallen bars.

- **A Space family in Generate** : eight themes built around the new pieces (Gravity Well,
  Starfield, Crystal Room, Dot Sea, Spectral Floor, Line Wall, Data Floor, Crossing), and
  a **Space** group of nine master chains that lay the whole picture on a floor, a wall,
  a ribbon or a horizon (Floor, Corridor, Ribbon, Horizon, Curved Screen, Data Wall,
  Halftone Floor, Scan Relief, Starlight). Generate now counts 108 themes, the chain menu
  109 chains.

- **Fulldome you can play** : every mapping slider (rotate, spin, feather, turns, top,
  bottom, azimuth, elevation, width, roll, surround, scale, offset) takes a modulator or
  a Meta knob, and turns the modulated color while it moves : a screen drifting around
  the room, a panorama breathing toward the zenith, a full-dome frame swelling on the
  beat. **spin** reaches 360°/s either way, a full turn a second, and never jumps when it
  changes. **feather** starts at 0, every edge crisp.

## Fixed

- **Fulldome** : **feather** now softens the picture's own edges in every mode (the
  screen's border, the panorama's top and bottom, a contained frame), not only the rim
  below the horizon; the projector window no longer shows the Mapping grid over a
  domemaster; in Screen mode the surround's top and bottom sit under their own heading
  (*behind the screen*); and changing **spin** no longer makes the dome jump.

- **A console flood, and a hidden cost, gone.** Reading the picture back (for the vision
  features, Sonify, the depth input and every output capture) made Chromium copy each
  read-back buffer into shared memory, a copy that was never used and that printed a
  performance warning each time, about thirteen times a second from the moment the app
  started. Besides the noise, it used up the browser's budget of console messages, so a
  real graphics error would have gone unreported. Now no copy is made : no warnings, and a
  projector or Spout capture costs about a third of what it did.

- **Generate never sweeps a whole-frame flip.** A theme's motion could bind Lowercase's
  `flip`, which inverts the whole frame on every swing : a strobe. It now joins the seed
  in the inputs no theme modulates.

## Opening an older session

Everything opens and plays as before. Two looks change on purpose :
- a dome mapped in **Screen** mode has a crisper border at feather 0 (it kept a hidden
  1.5 % softness), and a fresh dome starts at feather 0 instead of 0.01. A dome you saved
  keeps its own feather : double-click the slider to set it to 0;
- a dome that was spinning keeps its speed, and now turns smoothly when the speed changes.

## Install

| Platform | File |
|---|---|
| **Windows** | `Palinopsia-1.2.6-win-x64.exe` (installer) or `Palinopsia-1.2.6-portable.exe` |
| **macOS** (Apple Silicon) | `Palinopsia-1.2.6-mac-arm64.dmg` |
| **Linux** | `Palinopsia-1.2.6-linux-x86_64.AppImage` or `Palinopsia-1.2.6-linux-amd64.deb` |

Builds are unsigned :
- **Windows** : SmartScreen may warn, click **More info → Run anyway**.
- **macOS** (Apple Silicon) : not notarized. Drag Palinopsia into **Applications** first
  (run from the disk image or Downloads, macOS runs a hidden temporary copy), then run
  `xattr -cr /Applications/Palinopsia.app` once in Terminal, or open it and click **Open
  Anyway** in System Settings → Privacy & Security. The first launch asks for the local
  network, and for the camera and the microphone when you use them : accept, or OSC, NDI
  and light output stay silent. Screen capture is the one permission macOS never prompts
  for : switch Palinopsia on under Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording,
  then start it again.
- **Linux** : `chmod +x` the AppImage, then run it.

**Intel Macs (x64)** : this release ships Apple Silicon only. On an Intel Mac, build from
source (`npm install`, then `npm run build:mac`).

## For developers

- **Native generators can read the sound and another layer.** `NativeGenerator` gains an
  optional `feed(audio, side)`, called by the Compositor just before `render` : the shared
  128×2 audio texture and the slot's sidechain layer (the Background feeds the sound
  only). Lattice is the first to use it; the Inspector's sidechain row is a per-source
  entry with its own hint.
- **Generate recipes** gain `stackFx` (per-layer effects that close that layer's rack : the
  effect a theme cannot do without), `bgOpacity` (lower it when the layers carry their
  own dark ground : in group mode the Background shows through every dark part) and a
  motion recipe's `skip` (a RegExp of inputs that family never binds).
- **A new modulation target**, `{ kind: 'dome', field }`, over `DOME_MOD_RANGE` in
  `shared/dome.ts` : a final override on the dome stage through
  `Compositor.setDomeParam` / `domeParam`, the base back from the dome settings every
  frame.
- **Read-back buffers use `STREAM_COPY`**, never a `*_READ` usage : with a read usage
  Chromium makes a shadow copy at every fence, which these reads never used.
- **A GLSL lesson from Lowercase slant** : a texture read inside one branch of a figure
  switch made the compiler run every figure for every pixel (1.9 → 12.5 ms at 4K). ISF
  shaders are GLSL ES 1.00, with no gradient-free texture read in a fragment shader : read
  textures outside the branches.
