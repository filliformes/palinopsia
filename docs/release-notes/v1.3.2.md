**Palinopsia v1.3.2** (in progress)

## New

- **Every color can be modulated.** On any color picker, in a source, an effect, the
  Background or the finishing stages (the Vibe palette's stops too), the color's M binds
  its slider (the hue) and both axes of its pad (the saturation across, the brightness
  up), and the alpha. The Modulate panel switches between them; each part takes a
  modulator or a Meta knob like any slider. One part moves and the others hold, so a hue
  going round keeps its saturation and brightness. Over OSC, add `/hue`, `/saturation`,
  `/brightness` or `/alpha` to a color's address.

## Changed

- **The depth menu moved to the Performance tab's picture section**; Parallax, Pulfrich
  and Luma Blur say when depth is off, with a button that turns AI on.

## Fixed

- **Silhouette's trail works in Matte mode.** The body leaves grey echoes that fade to
  black behind it, like the trail in the other modes : a key that lingers.
- **Marbling's water keeps flowing on the dome and at 4K.** With evolve, comb and swirl
  turned up, the paint could slow to a few steps a second on a busy computer; now it
  stays at 60, and the drops look the same. With every setting at its top on a slow
  computer the water holds a little less paint instead of stepping.
