**Palinopsia v1.3.2** (in progress)

## New

- **The Macro knobs hide away.** A **MACRO** button at the right end of the Modulation
  row, next to ACTIVE, shows or hides the row of 16 knobs. It starts hidden (most of the
  time they are not played) and your computer remembers your choice; hidden, the picture
  gets the room back (about 170 px). The knobs keep playing while hidden : modulators,
  MIDI, OSC, Generate, Body rules and pads all still move them. A small dot on MACRO says
  when something is moving one, and its tooltip says what. MIDI Learn shows the row while
  it is on, and a knob picked in `Ctrl+K` brings it back; `Ctrl+K` → **Macro knobs**
  toggles it.
- **Every color can be modulated.** On any color picker, in a source, an effect, the
  Background or the finishing stages (the Vibe palette's stops too), the color's M binds
  its slider (the hue) and both axes of its pad (the saturation across, the brightness
  up), and the alpha. The Modulate panel switches between them; each part takes a
  modulator or a Macro knob like any slider. One part moves and the others hold, so a hue
  going round keeps its saturation and brightness. Over OSC, add `/hue`, `/saturation`,
  `/brightness` or `/alpha` to a color's address.

## Changed

- **The Meta knobs are now the Macro knobs** : the same 16 knobs, renamed everywhere
  you read them (the row, the Modulate panel, MIDI Learn, the action lists, `Ctrl+K`,
  the help and the docs). Over OSC they answer to `/opsia/macro/1` to `/16` (and
  `/opsia/randomize/macro`); the old `/opsia/meta/...` addresses keep working, both
  ways, so controllers mapped before do not notice. Sessions, MIDI maps and Body rules
  load as they were.
- **The depth menu moved to the Performance tab's picture section**; Parallax, Pulfrich
  and Luma Blur say when depth is off, with a button that turns AI on.

## Fixed

- **Silhouette's trail works in Matte mode.** The body leaves grey echoes that fade to
  black behind it, like the trail in the other modes : a key that lingers. A new **trail
  color** tints those echoes (the body stays white), with three new presets : Lingering
  key, Matte comet, Cold wake.
- **Marbling's water keeps flowing on the dome and at 4K.** With evolve, comb and swirl
  turned up, the paint could slow to a few steps a second on a busy computer; now it
  stays at 60, and the drops look the same. With every setting at its top on a slow
  computer the water holds a little less paint instead of stepping.
