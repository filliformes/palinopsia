**Palinopsia v1.3.2** (in progress)

## New

- **Sand Plate, a new source.** Sand on a vibrating metal plate : the grains bounce where
  the plate moves and gather along its still lines, in the looping figures you get on a real
  plate, thick at the crossings, with a few strays between. **order** sets how fine the
  figure is, **tones** and **warp** how far it strays from the textbook symmetry. With
  **audio** up, the loudest frequencies pick the figure, redrawn on the next accent, and the
  level shakes the plate : in silence the sand rests. Seven **surfaces** (black steel, spun
  metal, rust, slate, black glass, paper, felt) each hold the sand differently. **Rotation**
  turns the plate either way : the sand rides along, and spun fast it flies off in spiral
  rings. Drag on the preview to run a finger through it. With **plate opacity** at 0, only
  the sand stands over the layers below.

- **Signal can be played by the picture, like the Synth.** Signal's card has a new
  **PLAY** switch : **read heads** (as before) or **picture play**. In picture play the
  things moving in the picture play Signal's grains : where a thing is sets its pitch
  (by height, left to right, or from the center out), and the new **PICTURE** rows let
  its speed, size, brightness, color, texture and the rest shape every hit : how hard,
  how long, how bright, the wave, how often it ticks, where it sits and how likely it
  is to play. **strike** makes each moving thing tick grains on Signal's clock (free or
  synced) while it moves; **tie** holds one grain per thing while it moves, struck again
  at each new note. Signal still sounds like Signal : only who plays it changes. Sense,
  PICTURE and every row's depth take a modulator (their **M**), and the whole card
  takes a MIDI knob or an OSC fader through MIDI Learn.

- **Init : a blank session to start from.** The first entry of Generate (and the one
  selected when the app opens) gives you an empty session : four empty layers, no
  effects, the picture left as it is, and eight simple modulators already running (a
  slow sine, a triangle, a square, a saw, random steps, smooth random, an envelope and a
  ramp), ready to bind with the M beside any control. Pick a source on layer 1 and
  start.

- **Keep a Sonify you like through scene changes.** Right-click the Sonify button (or
  the Sonify page's header) → **Make current state persistent** : switching scenes no
  longer changes Sonify, even if they were saved with another one. The button turns
  gold; click **● persistent** on the page to let scenes change it again. **Set this
  Sonify in every scene** writes it into all of them instead.

- **A MIDI or OSC control can be a modulator.** Set a modulator to the new
  **controller** type, press **learn** on its card and move a fader, a knob or hit a pad
  (any MIDI device, TouchOSC, a phone) : everything bound to that modulator follows your
  hand, each parameter with its own depth, so one fader can move many things at once.
  A pad plays as an envelope (attack, release, how hard you hit). And **any modulator
  can be taken over** : its **✋** learns a control that takes the LFO (or whatever it
  is) over while you play it, then glides back to it a moment after you let go.

- **Black.** A new **■ Black** button at the far right of the top bar (key `.`) fades
  the whole show to black over the Morph time : every layer, the Background, the films'
  sound, Sonify and the Audio Layer, on the preview and every output and recording.
  Press it again and everything comes back over the same time. While it fades, a line
  runs clockwise round the button in exactly that time, so you see how long is left.
  It moves no fader :
  everything keeps playing underneath, so a scene you recall while black comes back
  with it. The button stays red while the show is out. It works from MIDI and OSC
  Learn, a Body rule, `Ctrl+K` and `/opsia/black` too.

- **A dice for Sonify's Reverb / Delay.** It rolls a new delay and a new reverb (Quartz
  or Prism, its size, decay, color and shimmer) and how loud each is in the tail; your
  send stays where it is (an off tail comes on, low).

- **OSC Learn : play Palinopsia from any OSC controller, in its own addresses.** MIDI
  Learn now learns OSC too : turn it on, click a control, then move a fader on TouchOSC,
  a slider in a Max patch or tilt your phone, and that address plays the control. No need
  to rename anything to `/opsia/…` : those addresses keep working as before, even while
  you learn, and a learned address never takes one of their places. If a message carries
  several values (a phone's tilt sends three), Palinopsia picks the one you moved. The
  range is found by itself (0 to 1, -1 to 1, 0 to 127, or whatever your control sends)
  and you can type it in the list. Buttons work like pads : one press, one step, and a
  message with no value fires each time. One fader can play several controls, as with
  MIDI. Give the controller a **reply port** in the list and Palinopsia answers it in its
  own address and range : your TouchOSC fader moves by itself when a scene comes back, a
  modulator sweeps the control or you Generate, like a motorized fader (it is left alone
  while your finger is on it).
- **Save your MIDI and OSC mappings with a session.** While Learn is on, a small
  **computer | session** switch beside it says where what you learn goes. **computer**
  is as before : the mapping works in every session on this computer. **session** saves
  it in the session file : a piece that needs its own controller setup keeps it, and
  another piece does not get it. Loading a session brings its mappings, New clears them,
  Generate keeps them (the same piece, a new look) and scenes never touch them. Where a
  session uses a knob or an address, its mapping wins over the computer's on that knob or
  address only; the list shows the computer's mapping greyed out, "replaced by this
  session", and every other mapping of the computer keeps working. Move any mapping from
  one side to the other in the list, or all at once (**move all to session**, **copy to
  computer**). The Macro knobs' MIDI CCs stay on the knobs, in the session, as before.

- **Fill the people with another layer.** The Silhouette effect has a **fill** : pick
  a layer at the top of its Inspector and that layer's picture is drawn where the people
  are, as if seen through a window shaped like them. In **cutout** they are made of it,
  in **hole** the film stays around them with the fill inside, in **shadow** the fill is
  tinted by the shadow color. **shade** keeps their own light and shadow on it so the
  body still reads. Mute the fill layer and you see it only inside them. With four
  presets (Bodies of light, Made of film, Stained figure, Wake) and a new Film Wall
  theme, **Inhabited** : the people in your films made of caustics, fire, dye or
  stripes.

- **Sonify has a Synth, and the picture plays it.** Turn on the new **Synth** voice and
  everything that moves in the picture becomes a player : each moving thing takes a voice
  (up to 8 at once, or one in mono), its place picks the note on your key and scale (low
  at the bottom; or left to right, or from the center out, on the dome from the zenith
  down), and a thing that keeps moving plays a melody, a new note at each step of the
  scale it crosses (or a glide). When it stops or leaves, its note lets go. How it looks
  shapes how it sounds : quick flicks pluck, slow drifts swell, its color moves the tone,
  its size the harmonics, its brightness opens the filter, it pans where it is. Open
  **picture** to rewire any of that (ten rows, each a destination, a source and a depth),
  or turn **PICTURE** down to let the picture pick only the notes. **score** turns the
  picture into a piano roll : a playhead sweeps it on the beat and the shapes it crosses
  play. Notes can wait for the 1/8, the 1/16 or the triplet. A ring on the mirror marks
  each thing that plays, with its note. The sound has eight engines to choose from (two
  digital oscillators that sync into a metallic edge, a hard-edged analog pair with ring
  modulation, a swarm, FM, struck resonators, a singing voice, a wavefolder through a
  low-pass gate, a plucked string), the same three knobs on each (timbre, harmonics,
  morph), three filters, chorus, eight voices (or all eight on one note, or one gliding),
  and 24 factory sounds to start from : the menu keeps the name of the one you picked
  (with "· edited" once you change it; pick it again to get it back). Its card reads like
  the Finishing panel, in colored sections (engine, read, tone, envelope, life, picture)
  that fold, with timbre, harmonics and morph drawn bigger as the main knobs, what each
  does on the engine beside it; the picture section has its own dice, which re-rolls only
  its ten rows (and always in a way that plays).

- **The Seq page holds every sequencer now.** One lane each, under one transport : the
  scene auto-pilot as you know it, the Sonify key sequencer (the same one as on the
  Sonify page, change it in either) and a brand new **Body rule timeline**. Press play
  and every lane marked "with transport" starts, the beat ones together on bar 1 (or on
  Link's next bar); stop stops them all. On the timeline, each of your Body rules has a
  row : drag its name onto the timeline, or draw, and the rule is on for that stretch and
  off outside it. Count it in **bars** of the tempo, or switch its clock to **time** :
  minutes and seconds the tempo never moves, starting the moment you press play (your
  blocks keep their place when you switch). Move and stretch the blocks with the mouse
  (Shift for beats or tenths of a second, Alt to copy), or double-click one to type its
  start and length exactly (`1:30.5`, or `5.3` for bar 5, beat 3). Loop 1 to 256 bars,
  or 5 seconds to 2 hours, or play it once; zoom in to the second on an hour-long one.
  Your hand still wins : turn a rule on or off with a pad, a knob or OSC while it runs
  and it keeps what you set until the next block edge on its row (a little **held** mark
  says so), then the timeline takes it back. The **live picture** sits at the top of the
  page, over every lane, so you always see what the sequencers do to it.

- **Your hand as a fader.** A body modulator can now take a **clutch** : a second body
  feature that says when it follows. Put the clutch on your left hand's openness and the
  modulator follows the hand only while it is open; close the hand and the value stays
  where you left it, wherever the hand goes after. Open it again and it glides back to
  the hand. Lose the hand from view and it holds too, so nothing ever jumps. Any feature,
  any parameter, above or below its threshold; a dot on the card shows when you have it.
  On the Body page, **Alt+click** a feature's → to make it the clutch.
- **A Body rule can play a layer's opacity**, each of the four and the Background's : off,
  full, off and back, a tenth up or down (MIDI learn has them too).
- **Silhouette is now an effect too.** Drop **Silhouette** (Utility, in the effect menu)
  on an imported film, a camera layer, an NDI input, anything, and it finds the people
  in that picture and cuts them out : the picture where they are and nothing else, a
  white matte to key another layer, a flat colored figure, or the picture with them
  taken away, with the source's trail and trail color. **over the picture** lays the
  result over the film (a figure painted onto it, ghosts left behind), **hold to the
  mask** holds the picture back a few frames so the cut fits a fast move. Up to four
  people. All the Silhouette effects share one body model and take turns, it rests when
  bypassed or on a muted layer, and it never opens a camera. With 15 presets, a **People**
  group of master chains and four new Film Wall themes (Cut Out, Shadow Theatre, Absent,
  Afterimages).
- **Turn Body rules on and off while you play** : learn a rule's box on a MIDI pad (each
  hit, on or off) or a knob, let another rule arm it, or send it over OSC.
- **The Macro knobs hide away.** A **MACRO** button at the right end of the Modulation
  row, next to ACTIVE, shows or hides the row of 16 knobs. It starts hidden (most of the
  time they are not played) and your computer remembers your choice; hidden, the picture
  gets the room back (about 170 px). The knobs keep playing while hidden : modulators,
  MIDI, OSC, Generate, Body rules and pads all still move them. A small dot on MACRO says
  when something is moving one, and its tooltip says what. MIDI Learn shows the row while
  it is on, and a knob picked in `Ctrl+K` brings it back; `Ctrl+K` → **Macro knobs**
  toggles it.
- **Every color can be modulated.** On any color picker, in a source, an effect, the
  Background or the finishing stages (the Vibe palette's stops too), the color's M binds
  its slider (the hue) and both axes of its pad (the saturation across, the brightness
  up), and the alpha. The Modulate panel switches between them; each part takes a
  modulator or a Macro knob like any slider. One part moves and the others hold, so a hue
  going round keeps its saturation and brightness. Over OSC, add `/hue`, `/saturation`,
  `/brightness` or `/alpha` to a color's address.

## Changed

- **Sonify's voices in five colored families.** **Spectrum** (Spectra, Filter),
  **wave** (Orbit, Raster, Transmission), **hits** (Flow, Events, Signal), **notes**
  (Chord, Synth) and **films** (Collage, Assemblage), then the Reverb / Delay. Each family
  has a header in its color that says which of its voices are on; click it to fold its
  cards (they keep playing). The Synth now sits between Chord and Collage. The mixer,
  the sequencer and Space list the voices in the same order (the mixer and the
  sequencer in the families' colors too).
  Sessions, mappings and the sequencer's steps are untouched.
- **Sonify starts with a little reverb and delay.** The Reverb / Delay card is on by
  default with its send at 0.05 : a touch of room on every voice from the first sound.
  Its on button and a double-click on the send bring 0.05 back. Chord and Collage start
  off, folded. Your sessions play as you saved them; the ↺ button brings the new
  defaults to this computer's Sonify.
- **An imported film plays at its own speed**, whatever the global speed (its
  `⊘ global` button starts lit; one click and it follows again). Sessions saved before
  play as they did.
- **Number boxes are only as wide as their numbers** : each fits the longest number its
  own range can show, so the label and the slider beside it get the room back (the
  Inspector's boxes are a third narrower). A box widens while you type a longer number.
- **The Meta knobs are now the Macro knobs** : the same 16 knobs, renamed everywhere
  you read them (the row, the Modulate panel, MIDI Learn, the action lists, `Ctrl+K`,
  the help and the docs). Over OSC they answer to `/opsia/macro/1` to `/16` (and
  `/opsia/randomize/macro`); the old `/opsia/meta/...` addresses keep working, both
  ways, so controllers mapped before do not notice. Sessions, MIDI maps and Body rules
  load as they were.
- **The depth menu moved to the Performance tab's picture section**; Parallax, Pulfrich
  and Luma Blur say when depth is off, with a button that turns AI on.

## Fixed

- **A modulator on the Synth's sense now works.** Its **M** was there and moved, but the
  Synth never heard it. (A MIDI knob on it always worked.)

- **The Sonify Synth plays in tune.** Its default picture patchbay bent every moving
  note by up to a third of a semitone (the bend row is now off; your saved sessions
  play in tune too). The FM engine no longer drops an octave in the middle of its
  harmonics knob, the second oscillator of the dco and ms engines sits on real
  intervals, and the same note no longer comes out much quieter one time than the next.
  Also fixed : old notes coming back when you switch the Synth on again, a note that
  could stay stuck when you picked a mono sound while playing, a release that ignored
  the knob while a note held, and unison being louder than poly on two engines.
- **Films keep playing on the projector when you minimize the controls.** With the
  control window minimized or hidden, the output, NDI, Spout and your recordings went
  on, but every film on them (a layer's film, a Collage wall, an Assemble edit) moved
  about once a second. Now they play at their own frame rate, as smoothly as with the
  window open, for no more work.
- **Sonify's cards stay inside their column when you narrow it.** The ♪ scale buttons,
  Events' blend and the reverb's freeze no longer stick out, and no slider gets too
  short to grab : a crowded row puts its controls on a line under its name, the Synth's
  envelope and patchbay go one slider per line, and the mixer's strips go on two lines.
- **A film keeps playing when you change the composition size** (or the render scale,
  or turn the dome on with its square) : it was swapped for Drift Field on layer 1 and
  started over on the others. Now it goes on where it was, without a flash. Cameras,
  NDI, HIVE and Assemble edits on layer 1 stay too.
- **Silhouette's trail works in Matte mode.** The body leaves grey echoes that fade to
  black behind it, like the trail in the other modes : a key that lingers. A new **trail
  color** tints those echoes (the body stays white), with three new presets : Lingering
  key, Matte comet, Cold wake.
- **Marbling's water keeps flowing on the dome and at 4K.** With evolve, comb and swirl
  turned up, the paint could slow to a few steps a second on a busy computer; now it
  stays at 60, and the drops look the same. With every setting at its top on a slow
  computer the water holds a little less paint instead of stepping.
