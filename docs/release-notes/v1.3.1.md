**Palinopsia v1.3.1 : paint, hands and steps.**

A release for playing with your hands. Marbling floats paint on water, and you play it by
clicking the preview or by pinching in front of the camera. The Body camera now reads
your fingers : ten hand shapes fire rules, and a rule can play almost anything in the
instrument. A new step sequencer brings conditional steps and swing to the modulators.
The points of Lattice, Ash and Particle Drift can turn into ink drops, seeds and torn
paper. Depth AI finally runs, offline, from inside the app, and Body, the Sequencer and
Sonify each open in a window of their own.

## New

- **Marbling.** A new source : drops of paint floating on water, flat colors with crisp
  edges, like marbled paper. Each new drop pushes the paint already there into rings and
  lobes, and the fuller the water, the smaller each new drop spreads. **Evolve** carries it
  all on slow currents, with a comb raked across now and then and a swirl drifting over
  it, so it moves like a film (at 0 the picture holds still). Drops can land by
  themselves, on the beat, or on the sound. Past a points budget the oldest, most
  stretched drops dissolve into the water, so it can run all night. Twelve presets, four
  new master chains (Marbled paper) and two Generate themes (Marbled Paper, Night Bath).
  It holds 60 fps at 1080p and 4K with 500 drops on the water.

- **Click the preview to play on it.** With Marbling on the picture, a click on the
  preview drops paint right where you clicked, holding keeps pouring it, dragging leaves
  a trail of drops; switch the click tool to drag a stylus or a comb through the paint,
  or to swirl it. It works on the big preview, at any UI zoom, through the keystone and
  even on the dome. Each gesture is one undo step and is kept in the session, so the
  picture comes back the way you left it. An OSC message can drop paint at a place too.

- **Pinch to paint.** On the Body page, **Pinch = click** turns each hand's pinch into a
  pointer : pinch to drop paint, hold the pinch to pour, move while pinching to trail or
  comb, open your fingers to let go. Both hands at once, mirrored with Mirror, with a ring
  on the preview showing where each hand points.

- **A step sequencer.** A new modulator, **steps**, in the rhythms family : sixteen bars
  you draw with the mouse, played one after the other on the clock (free, or on a BPM
  division that keeps to Ableton Link's beat). A click turns a step into a rest.
  Right-click a step to choose when it plays : always, by chance (10 to 90 %), on some
  passes only (1:2 plays every other time through, 2:4 the second of every four...), only
  in a fill or only outside one, only the first time through or every time but the first.
  Hold **FILL** for a fill (Shift-click keeps it on, and a Body rule or a MIDI pad can
  switch it). **SWG** pushes every second step late for a looser groove, **GLIDE** slides
  from one value to the next, **LEN** sets how many of the sixteen steps play, and a step
  that does not play can hold its value or drop to 0. Little marks under the steps show
  their conditions. Recall a scene with another pattern and its passes count again from
  its next step 1. The dice and the stepped Generate themes use it too.

- **Points that are not pills.** Lattice, Ash and Particle Drift have a new
  **irregular** control : at 0 the points are the round dots they always were, and as
  it rises each point becomes a shape of its own, no two alike : a soft wobble first,
  then ink drops, seeds and pollen with rounded lobes, scraps of torn paper with ragged
  edges and thin slivers, each turned its own way. **evolve** lets the shapes morph and
  tumble slowly (0 keeps them still). It works on every Lattice form; in Particle Drift
  the head takes the shape and its trail thins to a wisp behind it; Ash's flecks stay
  soft. Nine new presets show it (Seed
  floor, Pollen globe, Spore galaxy, Pollen, Ink in water, Paper scraps, Torn confetti,
  Ink drops, Seed fall), and the Orbit and Murmuration themes sometimes use it.

- **Every source and effect has at least ten presets.** Silhouette gets its first
  (cutout, matte, shadow and hole, some leaving echoes), and Shatter, Crawl, Store Zoom,
  Cloud Study and 14 older pieces are filled up : Grown, Veins, Slime Mold, Fluid,
  Audio, Metamorph, Differential, Aperture, the Finalizer and the five recipe nodes
  (Remap, Luma Blur, Gooey, Matte, Lookup). Picking one Slime Mold, Veins or Fluid
  preset after another no longer keeps the last one's zoom or ground.

- **A body plays the whole instrument.** A Body rule can now fire some 250
  actions, found in a searchable list : a layer's mute, solo, source, A↔B, blend, freeze
  and punch, the triggers of the effects you loaded (a Field Store's grab), the chains,
  scenes, Worlds and Generate themes, the Meta knobs, the modulators, the Feel, the tempo
  and the key, the Audio Layer and the output. A MIDI pad can learn any of them too (the
  MIDI section's learn list).

- **Hand shapes.** The Body camera now reads each hand's fingers : a curl per finger,
  the thumb's pinch to each fingertip, the hand's roll as you see it, which way the
  palm faces and how big the hand looks (how near the camera it is), 24 new features
  in the monitor's HAND SHAPE group, for modulators and over OSC. And ten shapes to
  fire rules, each hand on its own : fist, open, point, peace, thumb up, thumb down,
  ok, horns, call and gun. A shape fires once when you make it and hold it a moment,
  and again only after you let it go. The camera preview names the shape each hand
  holds.
- **Body, the Sequencer and Sonify can each open in a window of their own**, like the
  Output page : right-click their button, **Body in a window**, **Sequencer in a
  window** or **Sonify in a window**. Keep the camera and its rules, the auto-pilot, or
  the sound on another screen while you play. Closing the window closes the page, **⇲**
  puts it back in the app, `B`, `Q` and `S` bring the window forward, and the next time
  the page opens in the app as usual.
- **Your own master chains.** Right-click the Master FX strip and choose **Save chain as
  preset…** : give it a name, and the chain as it plays now is kept, every effect with
  its settings, plus the Vibe and Context. Your chains come first in the chain presets
  menu (**my chains**), in Ctrl+K and in the next / previous chain actions, and the menu
  shows the name while one plays. The same right-click renames or deletes them.

## Changed

- **Marbling's comb and swirl use far less of the computer.** At 1080p the water now
  carries about half the points for the same picture (a 4K picture is unchanged), slivers
  combed thinner than half a pixel dissolve into the water, and the points budget really
  holds : at evolve 1 with comb and swirl the water used to run away (up to three times
  the budget) and move only a few times a second; now it stays near the budget and moves
  smoothly at 60 fps. With comb and swirl at full the work per frame is halved at 1080p.

- **A lone hand is on its own side.** With one hand in view, a right hand is now R
  (it used to always be L), and it keeps its side while it stays in view. With two
  hands, nothing changes.

## Fixed

- **The euclid card no longer shows a scrollbar** when its clock is on a BPM division.
- **Depth AI works, and works offline.** The header's **depth: AI** now runs a depth
  model that ships inside the app (the download grows by about 66 MB) : no internet
  needed, nothing downloaded, ever. It never started before (the app refused to fetch it
  from the internet). It reads the picture on the graphics card, on a thread of its own,
  so the picture keeps its 60 fps (measured with it on, on the RTX 4070 laptop). A new
  **AI detail** in the same menu sets how finely it reads the picture (252, 392 or 518
  px), and the depth map holds steady instead of brightening and darkening each time
  something enters the frame. Building from source : run `npm run fetch:depth` once.
- **Right-click menus open right against their button.** The Output button's menu
  opened well above the button; it now opens just over it, lined up with it, and every
  right-click menu fits its window from its real size, at any UI zoom.
- **The A and B letters of a layer are centered in their buttons** (and the
  background's BG, and the Audio Layer's A and B).
- **Small dropdowns in the Inspector show their whole choice.** Luma Blur's Quality
  ("smooth"), Gooey's Key ("brightest channel"), Matte's, Corrode's, Toile's,
  Faultline's and Decimate's were cut; they are now as wide as their longest choice.
- **The Sequencer's resize edges follow the pointer at any UI zoom** (the inspector
  column and the live monitor ran ahead of the hand at 125 %).
- **The Assemblage source's Inspector has proper margins.** Its rows no longer touch
  the panel's edges.

- **The chain menu names the chain that plays.** After a Body rule, a MIDI pad, a scene,
  a Generate or the dice changes the master chain, the Master FX menu shows that chain
  (or nothing, when the effects match no chain) instead of the last one picked by hand.

## Opening an older session

Everything opens as before : **irregular** is 0, so Lattice, Ash and Particle Drift look
exactly as they did, and every modulator card stays what it was. Two things differ on
purpose :

- **A rule on a left-hand gesture** (pinch L and the others) used to fire for any lone
  hand. Now a lone right hand fires the R gesture : add the R version to the rule if you
  want either hand.
- **If depth is set to AI**, the depth effects (Parallax, Context's depth, the
  depth-aware effects) now get a real depth map, where before they got none.

## Install

| Platform | File |
|---|---|
| **Windows** | `Palinopsia-1.3.1-win-x64.exe` (installer) or `Palinopsia-1.3.1-portable.exe` |
| **macOS** (Apple Silicon) | `Palinopsia-1.3.1-mac-arm64.dmg` |
| **Linux** | `Palinopsia-1.3.1-linux-x86_64.AppImage` or `Palinopsia-1.3.1-linux-amd64.deb` |

The downloads are about 66 MB larger than 1.3.0 : the depth model now ships inside.

Builds are unsigned :
- **Windows** : SmartScreen may warn, click **More info → Run anyway**.
- **macOS** (Apple Silicon) : not notarized. Drag Palinopsia into **Applications** first,
  then run `xattr -cr /Applications/Palinopsia.app` once in Terminal, or open it and click
  **Open Anyway** in System Settings → Privacy & Security. The first launch asks for the
  local network, and for the camera and the microphone when you use them : accept, or
  OSC, NDI, Ableton Link and light output stay silent. Screen capture is the one
  permission macOS never prompts for : switch Palinopsia on under Privacy & Security →
  Screen & System Audio Recording, then start it again.
- **Linux** : `chmod +x` the AppImage, then run it.

**Intel Macs (x64)** : this release ships Apple Silicon only. On an Intel Mac, build from
source : `npm install`, `npm run fetch:depth`, then `npm run build:mac`.

## For developers

- **The depth engine** : `npm run fetch:depth` (`scripts/fetch-depth.mjs`) fetches the
  pinned Transformers.js, its onnxruntime-web build and Depth Anything V2 Small into
  `resources/depth` (never in git), checking each file's SHA-256; CI runs it, cached,
  before building. The model runs in `workers/depthWorker.ts` over `opsia-asset://depth/`
  with remote models off.
- **Clicking the preview** : a native generator that implements `pointer?(ev)` receives
  the preview's pointer events (down / move / up in composition uv, a pointer id per mouse
  or hand). Routing : the selected slot if it takes the pointer, else the topmost visible
  layer that does, the Background last. Marbling (`engine/MarbleSource.ts`, the model in
  `engine/marbleModel.ts`, stepped in `workers/marbleWorker.ts`) is the first.
- **The steps modulator** : the model and its sanitizer in `engine/stepSeq.ts`, the tick
  and the pass counters in `engine/modulation.ts` (`restartRuns`, `restartRunsAt(beat)`).
- **Hand shapes** : `engine/handShape.ts`, pure and testable without a camera; its
  thresholds sit at its top.
- **Actions** : `actions.ts` (`act:*` ids) and `triggerCatalog.ts` hold the vocabulary
  Body rules and MIDI learn share.
- **Page windows** : `PageWindow.tsx` serves the Output, Body, Sequencer and Sonify
  windows; `ContextMenu.tsx`'s `placeMenu` measures a menu before placing it.
- **Shared outlines** : `shaders/isf/lib/blob.glsl` (the `// @blob` include) draws the
  organic point shapes in the ISF shaders and the native Lattice.
