**Palinopsia v1.3.1** (in progress)

## New

- **Points that are not pills.** Lattice, Ash and Particle Drift have a new
  **irregular** control : at 0 the points are the round dots they always were, and as
  it rises each point becomes a shape of its own, no two alike : a soft wobble first,
  then ink drops, seeds and pollen with rounded lobes, scraps of torn paper with ragged
  edges and thin slivers, each turned its own way. **evolve** lets the shapes morph and
  tumble slowly (0 keeps them still). It works on every Lattice form, Particle Drift's
  trails sweep the shapes along, Ash's flecks stay soft. Nine new presets show it (Seed
  floor, Pollen globe, Spore galaxy, Pollen, Ink in water, Paper scraps, Torn confetti,
  Ink drops, Seed fall), and the Orbit and Murmuration themes sometimes use it.

- **Every source and effect has at least ten presets.** Silhouette gets its first
  (cutout, matte, shadow and hole, some leaving echoes), and Shatter, Crawl, Store Zoom,
  Cloud Study and 14 older pieces are filled up : Grown, Veins, Slime Mold, Fluid,
  Audio, Metamorph, Differential, Aperture, the Finalizer and the five recipe nodes
  (Remap, Luma Blur, Gooey, Matte, Lookup). Picking one Slime Mold, Veins or Fluid
  preset after another no longer keeps the last one's zoom or ground.

- **A body plays the whole instrument.** A Body rule can now fire some 250
  actions, found in a searchable list : a layer's mute, solo, source, A↔B, blend, freeze
  and punch, the triggers of the effects you loaded (a Field Store's grab), the chains,
  scenes, Worlds and Generate themes, the Meta knobs, the modulators, the Feel, the tempo
  and the key, the Audio Layer and the output. A MIDI pad can learn any of them too (the
  MIDI section's learn list).
