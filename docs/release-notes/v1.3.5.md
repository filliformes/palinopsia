**Palinopsia v1.3.5 : objects in hand.**

A release about depth and touch. Palinopsia now draws real 3D : the **Model** source (built-in shapes or your own glTF, OBJ, STL or PLY file), the **Grid Mesh**, the **Terrain**, the **Point Cloud** and **Depth Scan**, all turned with a drag and brought nearer with the wheel, and marked **3D** or **2.5D** in the menus. **Slick** spreads crude oil over the picture. MIDI now hears everything (a clock to follow, the pitch bend, aftertouch, and **catch** per mapping), many fast faders no longer drop the frame rate, envelopes can be fired by the sound or by another modulator, and the audio tab shows plainly what comes in, what the modulators hear and what goes out. A full audit before release fixed the rest.

## New

- **See what your audio is doing.** The audio tab (A) now reads in three plain blocks.
  **Input** : one meter per channel you picked, named with the interface's own numbers
  (pick inputs 3+4 and they read IN 3 and IN 4; a mono pick shows one), in dB, with a
  peak that holds a moment and a red **clip** light when the input hits full scale, plus a
  word that says **signal**, **silent** or **no device**. **Heard** : what the audio
  modulators follow, named (**level**, **change**, **hit**), and a live spectrum with the
  six bands drawn as zones under their names and frequencies (sub, bass, low-mid, mid,
  presence, air). Point at an audio modulator's HZ range and its band shows on the
  spectrum. **Output** : a meter beside the Sonify, Audio Layer and Monitor faders, then
  what leaves on each output channel (OUT 1, OUT 2, or the channels you picked) and a
  **limiter** light. A small dot in the top bar says whether the input hears something,
  even with the tab closed.

- **Follow a MIDI clock.** Turn on **follow clock** in the MIDI section and Palinopsia's
  BPM follows the clock of a drum machine, a sequencer or a DAW on the MIDI input, steady
  (it reads the last four beats, so a jittery cable does not make the tempo wobble) and
  quick to follow a tempo change. The clock's **Start** plays the Seq transport from the
  top, **Stop** stops it, and the modulators synced to the BPM fall on its bar. **CLK**
  lights beside the BPM. When Ableton Link is enabled with other apps linked, Link wins and
  the clock shows as ignored. With the clock out on too, the device downstream follows the
  same tempo and transport.
- **Catch for faders and knobs.** After a scene recall, a session load or a mouse drag,
  a fader no longer sits where its value is, and the first touch made the picture jump.
  Each fader or knob mapping can now be set to **catch** : it does nothing until you move it to
  the value, then follows (its row in the MIDI list says which way to go). **Jump** stays
  the default, so your setups play as before; a switch sets what new mappings start as.
- **The pitch bend and aftertouch, learnable.** A keyboard's pitch bend wheel (its middle
  is exactly half way), its aftertouch and each key's own pressure now learn onto any
  slider or knob, like a fader.

- **More of the show on a MIDI or OSC controller.** In MIDI Learn, the **Vary** and
  **Randomize** amounts (the two sliders beside their buttons), the **Audio Layer**'s
  volume and A/B mix, and every effect chain's **⏻** (each layer's FX, each source's
  FX, each Loop rack) are learnable now, beside the Master FX **chain** and the
  **finishing** switches. A knob or fader moves the sliders over their own range; a pad,
  a button or an OSC button flips a chain on its press. The two amounts stay this
  computer's, as before; the Audio Layer and the chains are the session's, and a
  controller's flip is one Ctrl+Z.

- **Envelopes the sound fires.** A Ramp or ADSR modulator can now start on its own : the
  new **↯** on its card turns it to what fires it. Pick **the sound** and a band (a kick
  in 40 to 120 Hz fires it, the hats far above leave it alone), or a clip's sound, or
  **another modulator** crossing a line. Two marks on one track set when it fires and
  when it is ready again, so a held loud sound fires once, and a **hold** keeps a
  stuttering hit to one fire. The signal shows live under the marks, and ↯ flashes at
  each fire. Old sessions play as before.
- **Turn and zoom the 3D objects with the mouse.** Drag a Model or a Grid Mesh on the
  preview and it turns in any direction, as in any 3D program, its spin carrying on from
  wherever you leave it. The wheel over it brings it nearer or sends it away. With
  several objects on screen, each answers only under the cursor; over empty picture the
  wheel does nothing. Each drag is one undo step, the way you turned it is saved with the
  session and its scenes, and a preset puts it back. It works on the dome preview too,
  and with a pinching hand.
- **OCTOCOSME, playable in Palinopsia.** A ready session plays one OCTOCOSME controller :
  its four voices are four Grid Mesh shapes (each voice's instrument picks the shape's
  look, its knobs turn and tear it, its sound makes it appear), and its general section
  drives a chain of effects in place of the installation's Resolume stage. See
  `docs/sessions/OCTOCOSME.md`.
- **A controller's mapping can travel with the session.** In OSC's **map** pane, an alias
  can be kept on this computer or saved with the session (**+ in session**, or one click
  on **PC** / **SESSION**).
- **dataflou wires can travel with the session too.** In the dataflou section's
  **connections**, each wire into Palinopsia shows **PC** or **SESSION** : one click moves
  it, and **new wires** picks where the ones you make go. A session's wire takes the
  place of this computer's on the same destination or source (those wait, struck
  through), and plays only in the universe it was made in. New starts without them,
  Generate keeps them, older sessions play as before.
- **Presets over OSC** : `…/source/A/preset 3` (or a preset's name) recalls a source's
  preset.
- **Grid Mesh : shapes that scribble and shatter.** A new 3D source beside Model. A shape
  made as a grid of points (a sphere, a capsule, a cone, a plane…) turns slowly inside a
  still field of noise that stretches, folds and tears it, and is drawn in one of twelve
  ways : as its surface, a wire cage, a cloud of points, or point after point as rings,
  ribbons and scribbles, a fan of slivers, a pile of shards. The sound can swell the
  tearing and throw it somewhere new on every hit, another layer can be its skin (it
  tears with the shape), and its real depth can drive Parallax and the other depth
  effects. Two lights over matte, toon and shiny materials, and plain blends (normal,
  add, screen, lighten, exclusion) for how its pieces pile up. Its first eight presets,
  **OCTOCOSME 1** to **8**, are the looks of the OCTOCOSME installation's visuals;
  fourteen general ones follow, with five **Mesh** master chains and four Generate
  themes (Mesh Scribbles, Shard Garden, Torn Skin, Sounding Mesh).
- **Slick : crude oil over the picture.** A new effect for any layer or the whole show.
  Whatever plays (a forest, a dancer, a sky) is slowly covered by a spreading black crude
  slick that smothers what it touches, black and wet in the middle, the petrol rainbow
  only at its thin edges, the way real slicks look. Click the preview to spill (hold to
  pour, drag for a trail), or let it spill on the beat, on the sound's hits, on a trigger
  from MIDI, OSC or a Body rule, or seep up by itself. Bring in a **tide** from an edge, or
  ride **coverage** across a piece from clean to fully coated; let it **drip**, **recede**
  (it leaves a stain) or **clean** it away. Thirteen presets, five **Slick** master chains
  and two new Generate themes, **Contamination** and **Black Tide**.
- **Model : a crude coat.** Any object, in any material, can be coated in crude oil :
  black and wet, the rainbow where the coat thins at its edges, oil running down it. New
  presets and the theme **Crude Idol**.
- **Terrain : oil on the water.** The lake can carry a black slick that spreads between
  the land, rainbow at its edges, lapping at the shores and staining the beach. Presets
  **Oil on the lake** and **Black coast**.

- **Terrain : a picture raised into a landscape.** A new source that turns another layer
  (a film, a figure, anything) or a land of its own (hills, ridges, dunes, a crater,
  islands) into solid, lit land seen across, raised by its brightness, its darkness or
  its depth. Cut it into terraces with contour lines like a survey map or a model cut
  from card, color it with the picture or a ramp (earth, snow peaks, topo map, night,
  desert, lava, paper), let a low sun throw long shadows over it, haze the horizon, flood
  it with a lake that mirrors the hills, and fly over it forever. Drag on the preview and
  the land gives under your hand, then heals. Three new Generate themes (Raised Land,
  Endless Land, Survey Map) and four Landscape master chains.
- **The audio modulator listens to a frequency band.** Its new **HZ** row (under SMOOTH)
  is a slider with two ends you drag, a low and a high frequency from 0 to 20000 Hz shown
  beside it : the level, the hits, the centroid and
  the rest are then read only inside it. A kick at 40 to 120 Hz drives one thing, the hats
  at 6000 to 16000 another. 0 to 20000 is the whole sound, as before.
- **Lights undo, the rig included.** A fixture added, moved, patched or deleted comes
  back with Ctrl+Z, like the looks and cues; the lights' on / off switch never does.

- **The Space line.** While Space drives Sonify's output, a small line beside the Sonify
  meter and in the Connect tab's Audio section says the mode and its channels (`objects
  1-26`). It turns amber when the output has too few channels and some voices are dropped,
  and a **stereo** button there goes back to the stereo mix in one click. The meter's
  tooltip says it reads the mix before Space.
- **Favorite sessions.** The **Open ▾** menu now starts with your **Favorites** : star
  the session on screen (**☆ Add this session to favorites**) or any recent one (its
  **☆**), and it stays there to open in one click, above the recent list. A favorite whose
  file has moved is flagged **file missing** instead of vanishing, and **×** takes one
  off. Each favorite is also a trigger (**FAVORITE 1**, **2**…), so a MIDI pad, an OSC
  control or a body gesture can bring a session back mid-show, without a dialog.
- **Point Cloud : the picture as points in space.** A new source that lifts another layer
  (or a picture of its own) into up to 640,000 points, each with the picture's color, raised
  by its depth or its brightness, and seen through a camera that drifts round it. The points
  hold their place as the picture moves. They splash out on the sound's hits or the beat and
  gather back, blow away past a line and form again, and part under your hand when you drag
  on the preview (or pinch, or stand in the Room). A scan plane can sweep through them.
  With depth: AI on, the cloud reads the depth of its own picture.
- **Depth Scan : a plane through the depth.** A new effect : a plane sweeps through the
  picture's depth and lights what it crosses, with a contour line where it meets the
  picture and a trail behind it, on its own, on the beat or by hand. Its **slice** mode keeps
  only a band of depth and drops the rest (transparent, dimmed, black, or as a matte). With
  depth off it reads the picture's brightness.
- Themes **Lifted Picture**, **Sand on the Beat**, **Wearing Away** and **Depth Sweep**, and
  a **Depth** group of Master FX chains.
- **Model : a 3D object on any layer.** A new source, the first in real 3D. Fifteen
  shapes are built in (sphere, torus, cylinders, cubes, plane, disc, cone, capsule, the
  five regular solids and a knot), or drop a model file on a layer : glTF / GLB, OBJ,
  STL or PLY, up to two million triangles, colors and texture included. Draw it solid,
  as a wireframe, as its edges, as points, or solid with its edges, in chrome, glass,
  clay, plastic, metal, soap film, foil, a lit sphere, flat color or its normals. Turn
  it, spin it, wobble it, let the sound swell it. Another layer can be its skin :
  wrapped round it, projected on it, reflected in it. And its real depth drives
  Parallax, Pulfrich and Luma Blur's depth focus, in place of the depth AI. On the dome
  it stays round, in the middle of the sky. With 23 presets, four **Objects** chains
  and eight Generate themes.
- **Model : fragments and dust.** The Model breaks into its triangles and comes back
  together : on the sound's onsets, on the beat (every beat up to every two bars, on
  Ableton Link's bars when it is on), on a trigger a MIDI note, an OSC message or a Body
  rule can fire, or held by hand with **broken**, to modulate for slow dissolves. The
  shards fly out along their faces, scatter, spin, fall or rise, drift, then gather back;
  **crumble** sends them one after another, and **dust** turns a share of them into fine
  grains that hang and drift before they gather. In every look (the wireframe splits along
  its edges, the points scatter like sand), on the built-in shapes and on files, and the
  true depth follows the pieces. Presets **Breathing glass**, **Beat shatter**, **Dust on
  the bar**, **Shards on the kick**, **Slow dissolve** and the theme **Fragments**.

## Changed

- **Glass flows.** Its **drift** no longer just slides the pane to the right : the glass
  wanders slowly and a soft current bends it as it goes. A new **evolve** bends the glass
  out of its classic patterns, into meandering ribbons and swirls that keep changing, one
  kind of glass melting into another. Three new presets : Liquid pane, Melting cathedral,
  Shifting glass.
- **A World picked over OSC applies only when it changes.** A controller that keeps
  sending its state no longer restarts the World's morph on every message;
  `/opsia/world/apply` applies it again on purpose.
- **Words on screen from anywhere.** `…/source/A/text "hello"` over OSC (or the Agent's
  `set_text`) writes what a Text source shows.
- **A small blue 3D mark** follows the 3D sources (Model, Grid Mesh, Point Cloud,
  Terrain) and the effects that read depth (Parallax, Depth Scan, Luma Blur, Pulfrich) in
  the source and effect menus, and a lighter **2.5D** mark follows Oil Cloud, Lattice and
  Relief, which look 3D on a flat picture. Type `3d` or `2.5d` in a menu to list them.
- **The sources that need something from you are grouped.** Assemblage, Collage (films)
  and Silhouette (the Body camera) sit together in the source menu under *needs
  material*, each tagged with what it needs.
- **A model file opens in plastic**, so its own colors and a layer worn as its skin read
  at once (chrome, the default for the built-in shapes, reflected the studio instead).
- **Context's light says when it lights the surface material.** With a surface drawn and
  the glow at 0 its header reads *no glow · lights the surface* instead of *off*, and
  **raking light** names what lights the relief.

- **The signal flow map reads as a flow.** The picture's path is a thick spine through
  large blocks, links are curves weighted by what they carry, and the map is calm at rest :
  secondary links appear when you pick a block, the control plane reaches its outputs in
  one stream, sound and lights in a river under the picture. A slow wave of light travels
  the spine (the **flow ∿** switch in the top bar), and the map shows titles only (the
  detail panel keeps the rest).

## Fixed

- **A cleaner app icon.** The black square around the ball is gone, its lines are brighter,
  and it stays sharp at every size. (Windows may show the old icon on existing shortcuts
  until it refreshes its icon cache.)
- **Several MIDI faders at once no longer make Palinopsia stutter.** Moving faders fast
  (even one) used to drop the picture to a few frames a second; now any number of faders,
  as fast as you like, keeps it at 60 fps.
- **Model files, and the media scheme's privileges.** The app's own file scheme had
  quietly lost its privileges in September (two registrations where Electron takes
  one); they are back, which is also what lets the Model read its files.
- **Macro knob CCs follow scene changes.** A CC learned on a Macro knob stays through a
  scene recall, a Generate, a World and the dice. A scene saved before the Learn used to
  bring its knobs back without the CC, and the controller stopped playing them.
- **A session no longer publishes things on your mesh for good.** Opening a session
  with dataflou wires used to add what they need to this computer's publish list,
  where it stayed after the session closed. Now those parameters are on the mesh only
  while that session is open (marked **session** in the publish list), and a session
  can never publish the actions that open another session or a favorite.
- **Grid Mesh level and color alpha behave as in the original patch.** Half level now
  shows about a quarter of the color over black (it showed about 6%), and a color with
  alpha 0.5 is half as strong, not an eighth. Level 1 with an opaque color looks the same
  as before.
- **Credits : the Grid Mesh's "tri grid" and "quad grid" draw modes** are named after
  the draw modes of Cycling '74's jit.gl.mesh (Max/Jitter), now credited in the notices.
