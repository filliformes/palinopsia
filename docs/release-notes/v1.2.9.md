**Palinopsia v1.2.9** (in progress : the title comes with the release)

## Fixed

- **Sonify no longer clicks.** The Chord's notes now move smoothly when its picture
  changes, when its number of voices changes or is modulated, and when it is switched
  on and off. The Collage voice's films fade in and out whenever they start, stop,
  change film, are hidden or come back, and no longer click under a busy, churning wall.
  A voice whose mixer filter is engaged no longer clicks when it is switched off.

## For developers

- **Fading an AudioParam** : schedule it a little ahead and use `setTargetAtTime`.
  `param.value` read on the main thread lags the audio thread (a curve started from it
  jumps), and a `linearRampToValueAtTime` on a param with no automation event yet ramps
  from time 0 (an instant jump). `collageVoice.ts` `fade()` does it right.
- **A media element's "ready" is not "sounding"** : its sound reaches the graph tens of
  milliseconds after `play()`; `CollageVoice.sounding()` waits until its position has
  moved on.
- **Never nudge `playbackRate` for sync** : every change restarts the element's
  pitch-keeping time-stretch, a click; re-sync with a crossfaded splice instead.
