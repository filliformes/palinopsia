**Palinopsia v1.3.4** (in progress)

## New

### World and Feel

- **World and Feel, clear to use.**
  - **You can see what Feel is doing.** A dot on the feel tab while a dial is off rest
    (its hover lists them); a line under the Mixer's header saying what Density leaves of
    each layer's fader; a live line on each Finishing stage (Vibe, Context, Finalizer)
    with the values the picture really gets, the dials adding them and a **rest** button.
    Tonicity greys while the audio input is off, Superimposition under two visible layers.
  - **The World amount is a performance fader.** Beside the World menu in the Transport
    (**amt**, with Learn and its M) as well as in the World page; saved with the session
    and undoable (it was this computer's).
  - **A World says why it does little.** An amber line under *apply sets* : the audio
    input off (**turn audio on**), the layers its coupling moves that have no B source,
    its film not playing (**apply it again**). The Transport's menu reads *· film off*
    on the active World when New or launch started it without its film.
  - **Proximity joins Feel.** Saved with the session (it was this computer's), undoable,
    reset with the others, OSC `/opsia/proximity/follow` for its ◑; its Transport slot
    is the World amount's now. dataflou gains Tonicity, Shutter, Superimposition and the
    World amount.
### Audio

- **Latency, for sound and cameras.** In the Audio section, the audio engine's sample
  rate (the device's, or 44.1 to 96 kHz) and the buffer asked of the device (auto, or
  128 to 4096 samples), for Sonify (with the Audio Layer and a recording's sound) and
  the input with its monitor, with a line saying what the devices really give. In a
  camera's framing, its resolution and frame rate, kept per camera and applied at once,
  with what it delivers beside them : a lower resolution at a higher frame rate is
  often much quicker than a webcam's highest.
- **Pick an interface's channels.** In the Connect tab's Audio section, each device gets
  a channel menu : a stereo pair (1+2, 3+4…) or one channel (mono). The input's pick
  feeds the analysis, the monitor, learn noise and the Filter voice's line-in. Sonify's
  output (with the Audio Layer) and the monitor's output land on the picked pair of a
  device with more channels, or are summed onto one. Kept per computer; 1+2 as before
  until you pick. The Filter voice's line-in now opens the input chosen in the Audio
  section (it always opened the default input).
### OSC

- **Send to several places at once.** Under ↗ out, add a destination for each receiver :
  Pandore, dataFLOU, TouchDesigner… Each has its own address and port, and its own choice
  of what it gets.
  - **F** is the live state, everything or only some addresses (for example only
    `/opsia/layer1`).
  - **M** is MARK.
  - **B** is the Body rules' messages.

  Your current destination carries over as the first one. Palinopsia never sends into
  its own input.
- **See who is talking.** **links**, beside monitor, lists every program and device
  sending to Palinopsia, with its rate and when it was last heard. Open one to see each
  address it sends, with its last value and what Palinopsia does with it : understood,
  learned, mapped, or **not mapped** with a button to map it. Below, every place
  Palinopsia sends to.
- **Catch what is in the air.** Join a multicast group under ↗ in, and the messages
  devices send to it on your network reach Palinopsia, ready to map. A sender that floods
  the network can be muted in **links**.
- **One map of every address.** **map** brings together, with a filter :
  - **aliases**, where you send any controller address to any Palinopsia address, with
    its range, curve and invert;
  - what **OSC Learn** learned, with its range editable;
  - all of Palinopsia's own addresses.

### Sharing and support

- **What you make is yours.** Shaders, presets, recordings, and Sonify generators,
  mappers and effects belong to whoever makes them. To share one, list it in the new
  community catalog with a pull request : it stays in your own repository, under your
  name (see `CONTRIBUTING.md`).
- **☕ in the top bar.** Palinopsia is free and made by one person : the cup opens a page
  where you can support it.

## Changed

- **Randomize All re-rolls the Background** while it plays a source (a new source and
  rack; its opacity, speed, depth and blend stay yours; an empty background stays
  empty). It used to keep the ground, so All looked like a variation over the same
  background.
- **Feel's right sides no longer all blur.** Gesture ⇄ Texture, Coalesce and Interruption ⇄
  Flow each added blur or trails on their right side, so they all ended up soft. Now :
  **Texture** lays the picture on a lit material that slowly moves (the one chosen in
  Context, else paper), **mass** (Coalesce) bands the Vibe palette into flat fields of
  color, **flow** keeps its trails and stays sharp. None of them writes blur any more.
- **Feel's labels say what the dials do.** "Interruption ⇄ Flow" (its interruption end is
  on the left), Shutter's ends read off and 24 fps (just past off is the choppiest, 2 fps),
  Gesture ⇄ Texture reads crisp ⇄ smeared, the header says "whole-picture dials", one name
  for Coalesce everywhere, Monomedia's description matches what it sets, OSC Proximity reads
  far ⇄ close. **Drift is now Wander** (`/opsia/wander`; `/opsia/drift` and its MIDI
  binding still answer). The World page's subtitle says what a World is.
- **Generate no longer turns Shutter or Superimposition on** (a session never kept them, so
  the look did not come back when it was opened again); it sets both to 0.
- **The whole Audio section folds** to its header with its ▶, like MIDI and OSC (it
  keeps listening and playing; remembered on this computer).
- **The top bar's MIDI Learn button is now just Learn.** It learns MIDI and OSC
  controllers alike; the L key and everything it does are unchanged.
- **Output EQ and Glue : off by default, and they fold.** In the Connect tab's audio
  section, the Output EQ now starts off like the Glue, and each card folds to its header
  with a ▶ (folded at first, remembered on this computer). A session saved with the EQ on
  keeps it on.
- **dataflou : one node, and you choose what it shows.** The second node,
  `<name>-session`, is gone. Under **publish** in the dataflou section, tick whole groups
  (Layers, Feel, Scenes, the sources, each heading of the actions…) or open them to tick
  single parameters, and watch a gauge show how full the node is. A new install, and this
  update, start with the 16 Macro knobs only. Wiring one of Palinopsia's parameters from
  the section publishes it for you.

- **Safer on the network.**
  - Electron is updated with six releases of security fixes.
  - The OSCQuery port now only answers this computer, never a web page.
  - A HIVE viewer that stops reading is dropped instead of filling memory.
  - A shader's name can no longer save it outside its folder.

- **Datamosh is rewritten, and Palinopsia's own.** It reads motion more precisely, and
  keeps its controls, its presets and its look : the sticky blocks, the melt, the bloom
  on a cut, the actants.

- **Sonify's reverb is new, and Palinopsia's own.** It is written from the published
  reverb literature, with every length and constant its own. Its two modes are now
  **Nappe** (a dense pad) and **Strates** (three bands, each with its own decay). The
  knobs, the OSC addresses and your saved choice stay the same.

- **Credits, all in one place.** `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` now lists everything Palinopsia
  borrows: code, programs, models and their training data, fonts, textures, and the ideas
  and research behind its features. The license texts ship in `licenses/`, beside the app.
  A check run before every build keeps the list complete.

## Fixed

- **The Windows shortcuts' icon.** The desktop and Start menu shortcuts (and so the taskbar)
  showed a blank page instead of Palinopsia's icon : the installer wrote the app's long
  description into each shortcut, which Windows cut short while keeping its length, so
  every field after it (the icon too) was read shifted. The description is one short
  sentence now, and a release build refuses a long one again.
- **Tracking** : the freeze line and the chroma bleed are rewritten as Palinopsia's own
  code (no more lines from a CC BY 3.0 shader). The knobs are the same, and the bleed now
  trails to the right like a real tape's.
