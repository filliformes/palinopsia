**Palinopsia v1.3.4** (in progress)

## New

## Changed

- **Credits, all in one place.** `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` now lists everything Palinopsia
  borrows: code, programs, models and their training data, fonts, textures, and the ideas
  and research behind its features. The license texts ship in `licenses/`, beside the app.
  A check run before every build keeps the list complete.

## Fixed

- **Tracking** : the freeze line and the chroma bleed are rewritten as Palinopsia's own
  code (no more lines from a CC BY 3.0 shader). The knobs are the same, and the bleed now
  trails to the right like a real tape's.
