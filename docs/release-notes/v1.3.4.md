**Palinopsia v1.3.4** (in progress)

## New

### OSC

- **Send to several places at once.** Under ↗ out, add a destination for each receiver :
  Pandore, dataFLOU, TouchDesigner… Each has its own address and port, and its own choice
  of what it gets.
  - **F** is the live state, everything or only some addresses (for example only
    `/opsia/layer1`).
  - **M** is MARK.
  - **B** is the Body rules' messages.

  Your current destination carries over as the first one. Palinopsia never sends into
  its own input.
- **See who is talking.** **links**, beside monitor, lists every program and device
  sending to Palinopsia, with its rate and when it was last heard. Open one to see each
  address it sends, with its last value and what Palinopsia does with it : understood,
  learned, mapped, or **not mapped** with a button to map it. Below, every place
  Palinopsia sends to.
- **One map of every address.** **map** brings together, with a filter :
  - **aliases**, where you send any controller address to any Palinopsia address, with
    its range, curve and invert;
  - what **OSC Learn** learned, with its range editable;
  - all of Palinopsia's own addresses.

### Sharing and support

- **What you make is yours.** Shaders, presets, recordings, and Sonify generators,
  mappers and effects belong to whoever makes them. To share one, list it in the new
  community catalog with a pull request : it stays in your own repository, under your
  name (see `CONTRIBUTING.md`).
- **☕ in the top bar.** Palinopsia is free and made by one person : the cup opens a page
  where you can support it.

## Changed

- **dataflou : one node, and you choose what it shows.** The second node,
  `<name>-session`, is gone. Under **publish** in the dataflou section, tick whole groups
  (Layers, Feel, Scenes, the sources, each heading of the actions…) or open them to tick
  single parameters, and watch a gauge show how full the node is. A new install, and this
  update, start with the 16 Macro knobs only. Wiring one of Palinopsia's parameters from
  the section publishes it for you.

- **Safer on the network.**
  - Electron is updated with six releases of security fixes.
  - The OSCQuery port now only answers this computer, never a web page.
  - A HIVE viewer that stops reading is dropped instead of filling memory.
  - A shader's name can no longer save it outside its folder.

- **Sonify's reverb is new, and Palinopsia's own.** It is written from the published
  reverb literature, with every length and constant its own. Its two modes are now
  **Nappe** (a dense pad) and **Strates** (three bands, each with its own decay). The
  knobs, the OSC addresses and your saved choice stay the same.

- **Credits, all in one place.** `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` now lists everything Palinopsia
  borrows: code, programs, models and their training data, fonts, textures, and the ideas
  and research behind its features. The license texts ship in `licenses/`, beside the app.
  A check run before every build keeps the list complete.

## Fixed

- **Tracking** : the freeze line and the chroma bleed are rewritten as Palinopsia's own
  code (no more lines from a CC BY 3.0 shader). The knobs are the same, and the bleed now
  trails to the right like a real tape's.
