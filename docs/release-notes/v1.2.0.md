**Palinopsia v1.2.0 : the dome and the grain.**

Palinopsia goes under the dome and out to the rest of the video world : a real fulldome output
(210°, up to 4K), NDI and Syphon built in, and real-time DXV3 recording at dome size. Down at
the grain, surfaces grow, crack and rust (Grown, Colony, Ground, Scan), film gets damaged the
way real film does, and CRT / VHS faults look like the real thing. The camera learns how a body
moves and can cut it out of the room, Generate builds scenes that move, and a video plays at
any speed from 1/64× to 64×. And every source, effect, node and finishing stage went through a
deep audit : what was broken is fixed, and what gave the instrument its character is kept, as
the default look, with the clean version as an option.

## Highlights

- **Fulldome output.** A square domemaster (front at the bottom), 2K or 4K, aperture
  180 to 230°, feeding everything : preview, projector, NDI, Spout, Syphon, recording and
  stills. Fit a flat picture as *full dome*, *panorama* or *screen*, and check it in a 3D
  dome simulator, from the seat or from outside.
- **NDI in and out, built in.** Any NDI source on the network (a camera, a phone, OBS,
  another Palinopsia) plays in a layer like a webcam : **NDI Input…** in the source menu.
  Always the newest frame, so it never drifts behind (a 1080p source arrives in ~45 ms).
  And Palinopsia is its own NDI source : 4096×4096 at 30 fps, even with the
  window minimized. No NDI on the computer? One click installs NDI's official runtime.
- **Syphon on macOS**, the twin of Spout.
- **DXV3 recording in real time** : the GPU compresses each frame and the file is written as
  it goes, so the 4096² dome master records at full size, with nothing to convert after.
- **Living surfaces.** Four new Organic generators : **Grown** (a texture that grows itself
  and heals when you damage it), **Colony** (lichen, mold, burning paper, rust), **Ground**
  (drying mud, migrating sand ripples, strata, wood, bark) and **Scan** (30 real
  photographed materials). The rest of the Organic family is now lit as a relief.
- **Film damage, rebuilt from real film** : dust, fibers, scratches and a hair in the gate,
  per film gauge, plus a **dirt burst** you can fire on a beat.
- **The camera reads movement** : a **motion field** (which way things move, whether they
  spread out or close in, turn, move as one), six **movement qualities** of a body (energy,
  expansion, fluidity, suddenness, directness, symmetry, plus impulse and freeze gestures),
  and a **Silhouette** source that cuts the body out of the room, no green screen.
- **Generate builds scenes that move**, using the whole instrument, with three new
  families (Dome, Film Wall, Node Workshop) and 25 new master chains.
- **Video, any speed, and a real transport** : 1/64× to 64× on ordinary footage, stop, scrub
  the timeline, and step through the other clips in the folder.
- **A folder of sessions** links into the Session dropdown in one right-click.
- **Resolume OSC mapper** (key `K`) : open a Resolume composition and wire Palinopsia's
  signals to its layers, clips and effects in a matrix.
- **The big audit** : all 23 generators, the native sources, 52 effects, the finishing
  chain and all 25 native nodes. Knobs never jump the picture anymore, long shows stay
  alive, and transparency, 4K and the dome are handled everywhere.

## Your sessions are safe

- **A session file only changes when you save it.** Loading another session, Open,
  New, Generate and quitting used to save the session you were leaving into its file
  without asking; after a crash restore it could even land in ANOTHER session's file
  that shared its name. Now they ask **Save changes?** (Save · Don't save · Cancel),
  and only when something changed. Don't save keeps a recovery copy.
- **Every overwrite keeps the previous version** : right-click **Load** → **Earlier
  versions** brings one back. The crash autosave keeps the last hour, and **Restore**
  reconnects to the session's file.

## Opening an older session : what may look different

Most sessions open exactly as before. These changes are deliberate, and each one either
fixes something that did not work or can be set back :

- **Finalizer "A" is now "opacity"**, a real fade to black. It used to do nothing, so a
  session saved with it below 1 now looks darker : set it back to 1.
- **Context's material relief finally shows.** Picking a material (bricks, bark, paper…)
  now relieves the picture with that material; before, it read a random image instead.
  The same bug made Vibe and Context skip frames, even with no material : that is gone.
- **Light Trails and Wide Time fade all the way to black** instead of leaving a permanent
  fog. Wide Time's blown-out look is kept as its new `burn` mode.
- **Wavefold and Colorizer** no longer turn dark grounds white by default; the old
  negative look is the new `invert` switch.
- **Melt** creeps visibly now, and **Corrode** eats the frame over minutes (one minute to an
  hour) instead of seconds.
- **Pixel Sort's length** works past 96 pixels.
- **The heart shape** (Transform and the output shape) stands upright with a clean edge. It
  used to be upside down : rotate by 180° for the old one.
- **Weave, lumakey and consume** now do something as layer blend modes (they acted as
  normal before).
- **Flash safety is on** (mild, 0.35) unless you set it yourself : a bug left it off on
  installs that had never touched it. It now really catches strobes, and on a very bright
  scene it can soften film flutter too.
- **Randomize leaves deliberate settings alone** : anaglyph 3D, film hold, the output shape,
  Vibe's chord mode and all the film damage are never rolled.
- Sessions saved before the film-damage rebuild with Film Hold off open with dust and
  scratch at zero, once (they were invisible before, so nothing you saw changes).
- **A dome master saved at 8K opens at 4K** : the live dome now stops at 4K (see Fixed).
- **Seven surface materials keep their real proportions** : bricks, concrete, corrugated
  steel, painted plaster, rock face, wood grain and paper crumpled used to be stretched to
  about twice their height, in Context's surface material and in the Scan source. They now
  look like the real thing, so their features are wider and flatter than before.
  **Rock rough** is renamed **stacked stone** (what it is); sessions keep it.
- **Video grain plays the right way up** : grain voices were upside down.

## New

### Fulldome (Output page)

- A **domemaster** at 2K or 4K, equidistant fisheye, front at the bottom, aperture 180
  to 230° (210° by default, as in the SAT Satosphère). It replaces the frame everywhere :
  preview, projector window, NDI, Spout, Syphon, recording and stills.
- Three ways to fit a flat picture :
  - **full dome** : the whole frame over the whole dome, nothing cropped, no black (or
    cover / contain);
  - **panorama** : wrapped around the room, up to the zenith;
  - **screen** : a flat virtual screen, re-projected so it reads undistorted from the
    center.
- Rotate, spin, rim feather, flip, and an alignment grid burned into the output.
- A **3D dome simulator** shows the live master on a dome : from the seat or as an outside
  cutaway, with tilt, a template and the sweet spot, and a "whole dome" view.
- The dome is off by default, and the section has a reset-to-defaults button.

### Output and recording

- **NDI output**, built in, replacing the old optional stub. Sends the clean picture or the
  domemaster, 4096×4096 at 30 fps (measured into a receiver, with a GPU-heavy scene and the
  window minimized). Name, frame rate (25 to 60, 29.97 and 59.94 exact), size cap, UYVY or
  RGB. For venue networks : Discovery Server, network card, extra IPs and groups, applied
  live. Receiver status, sent rate and an ON AIR / PREVIEW tally.
- **One-click NDI runtime install** on a computer without NDI (Windows and macOS) :
  downloaded from NDI over HTTPS, its signature checked, NDI's own installer opened, and
  the source goes live by itself when the install finishes.
- **Syphon output (macOS)** : a Syphon server named Palinopsia, for any Syphon app on the
  same Mac.
- **DXV3 recording in real time.** The graphics card compresses each frame and a few
  workers write the file as it goes : no conversion afterwards and no encoder size limit,
  so the 4096² dome master records at full size (larger masters are scaled to 4096).
  Constant 30 or 60 fps, the clean picture. Validated with FFmpeg's DXV decoder, in the
  same file layout as Resolume's own clips.
- **Record location** : Output → Record → **location…** picks where takes, screenshots and
  Assemble exports go. It is checked, remembered, and if the folder can't be reached (an
  unplugged drive) the take falls back to Recorded/ with a warning instead of being lost.
- **Recording formats know their limits.** The hardware video encoder stops at 3840×2160,
  so above that (the 4K dome) the other formats are grayed out and the take records DXV3.
  The MIDI record toggle now uses the chosen format too. "Fast · no re-encode" is now
  labeled for what it is : MKV, H.264 as captured.

### Organic : surfaces that live

- **Grown** : a texture that grows itself. A tiny neural cellular automaton, trained on a
  real photographed surface (lava, mossy rock, bark), grows it cell by cell from nothing
  and keeps it alive. **DAMAGE** cuts a hole that heals within a second, **REGROW** starts
  over. Six presets.
- **Colony** : living matter spreading across a surface, in four kinds : lichen on granite,
  mold on agar, burning paper, rust on steel (or verdigris on copper). Fronts grow
  roughly, slow on poor ground and stop short of each other; each colony ages (lichen
  cracks, mold rings, char turns to ash, rust pits and flakes). Regrow on a button or a
  cycle. Ten presets.
- **Ground** : the surfaces under the living things. Mud drying and cracking, sand ripples
  migrating with the wind, rock strata, wood end grain and bark, each with an alternate
  palette. Ten presets.
- **Scan** : 30 real photographed material scans laid across the frame without visible
  repeats, relit by the Organic light. Colony's transparent `ground` grows lichen or rust
  over it. Ten presets.
- **Veins** : a leaf blade growing and calling its veins (open or looped), or roots
  reaching down through the soil, by the model botanists use to reproduce real leaves.
- **Mycelium grows for real** : hyphal tips that branch, fuse and eat the ground (it was
  noise contours). Same controls, so sessions keep theirs.
- **Slime Mould** : a slime mould growing its transport network from hundreds of
  thousands of agents : veins that thicken, prune, reroute and forage.
- **Fluid** : a real fluid solved live : ink blooming in water, smoke rising into eddies,
  fire glowing by its temperature with soot above it. Seven presets.
- **Reaction, rebuilt at full precision** : the same pattern at any resolution and frame
  rate, `rate` up to 3, `scale` reshapes it live instead of restarting, and **regrow ▸**.
- **The Organic family, made physical** : an Organic section in the source picker; **relief
  lighting** on Reaction, Erosion, Membrane, Mycelium and Dye Field (`relief` and `light
  angle`, 0 for the old flat print); **Swell** rebuilt from real deep-water wave physics;
  **water** caustics focused by real waves; **fire** that stretches as it rises, flickers
  with a real rhythm and is colored by temperature.

### Film and analog finishing

- **Film dust and scratch, rebuilt from how real film gets damaged.** Dust changes every
  film frame (24 fps, 18 on Super 8), mostly tiny specks and the odd big clump or fiber,
  dark on the print or white from the negative. Scratches run along the strip for a
  fraction of a second to a minute, wander, break up and fade; mostly dark, some white or
  green-yellow. It now works with Film Hold off, and costs under half a millisecond at
  4096².
- New film controls : **gate hair** (a hair caught in the projector gate), **film gauge**
  (35 mm, 16 mm, Super 8), **dirt on** (print, mixed, negative), **dirt burst ▸** (a dirty
  stretch of film, fire it on a beat or an onset, even with dust at 0) and **film grab ▸**
  (re-draw or re-freeze the hand-made film on a trigger).
- **CRT / VHS parasites, rebuilt** in real scanlines. VHS : wobbling line edges, a frayed
  head-switch tear, white dropouts with a recovery tail, and a tracking band of torn,
  snowy lines when pushed. CRT : a rolling hum bar, a faint RF weave and impulse specks.

### Body and vision (Body page)

- **Motion field** (**Motion** toggle) : the camera's optical flow read as nine features :
  how much moves, which way, whether it **spreads out or closes in** (someone approaching,
  arms opening), whether it **turns**, whether it moves **as one or every which way**,
  where, and how much of the frame. Gestures : swipe left / right / up / down, approach,
  withdraw, turn, and **stillness**. It follows anything that moves, a crowd or a curtain
  as well as a body. The picture itself gets the same reading on the vision bus.
- **Movement qualities** (with Pose on) : how the body moves, not only where. **Energy**,
  **expansion**, **fluidity**, **suddenness**, **directness** and **symmetry**, measured
  against the body's own size so near and far read alike, with two gestures : **impulse**
  (a burst out of calm) and **freeze** (a quick stop held still). The camera's own jitter
  is learned and taken off, so a still body reads still on any camera.
- **Silhouette source** (with the live inputs in the source picker) : **cutout** (the
  camera where you are, nothing elsewhere), **matte** (white on black, to key or feed a
  Matte node), **shadow** (a flat colored body) or **hole** (the room with you taken out),
  with a **trail** of fading echoes. It never turns the camera on by itself.
- Every body feature streams over OSC as `/opsia/body/<feature>`.
- **Tracking runs once per camera frame**, about half the cost it was; **Hi-res** runs it on
  every display frame for a fast camera or the finest landmarks.

### Generate and master chains

- **Generated scenes move** : each theme binds a few modulators across its layers in its
  family's way of moving (slow breathing for Organic, drawn cadences for film, stepped and
  on the beat for Glitch, sine and saw sweeps for Analog).
- **Generate uses the whole instrument** : sources, effects, native nodes and blend modes
  it never picked now land, and nodes that read another layer get a layer in the scene.
  A new **Living Surfaces** family (Lichen, Rust, Mold Culture, Burning Paper, Dry Earth,
  Grown).
- Three more families : **Dome** (made for a fulldome : no vignette, nothing framing the
  edges, slow motion overhead), **Film Wall** (your own films through a Collage, graded and
  damaged) and **Node Workshop** (one recipe node per theme). **Test** is now **Feel
  Studies**, at the end of the list.
- **25 new master chains** in three groups : **Time & memory**, **Node recipes** (layer-
  reading nodes wired to layers in use) and **Dome** (dome-safe : Context's vignette off,
  nothing framing the edges). Chain presets can now say which layer a node reads.
- **An Analog section** in the source pickers : RGB Oscillators, Sync Osc, Slit Scan,
  Ramps, Column Scan, Differential and Interference.

### Video

- **Any speed** : slow motion down to **1/64×** and fast up to **64×**, forward and in
  reverse, on ordinary footage. When a clip's seeks run slow, the player steps keyframe to
  keyframe (ffmpeg indexes each clip's keyframes once, in the background). The **◇ smooth**
  copy still plays the smoothest, and its button lights up when the settings will strobe.
- **■ stop** (pause and back to the in point), **scrub** the timeline playing or paused
  (the playhead lights up while dragged), and a **clip** row (◀ / ▶ and a dropdown) that
  steps through the other videos in the clip's folder, keeping speed, direction, loop and
  grain.

### Sessions, modulation and finishing

- **Link a folder of sessions** : right-click the Session **Load** button and pick a
  folder; every session in it (subfolders included) joins the dropdown, grouped under the
  folder's name. Linked, not copied : sessions saved there later show up too.
- **Click a modulation chip** (Modulation panel) to turn that assignment off and on; it
  keeps its depth meanwhile.
- **↺ default** on Vibe, Context and Finalizer puts the stage back as a New session starts
  it.
- **Context shows what the picture really gets** : when Proximity, Coalesce, Flow or the
  sequencer add blur, haze, trails or depth on top of its sliders, a line under Context's
  header says so (`live blur 0.23 ← Proximity`), and **neutral** sets those macros back.
- **New and Generate morph** into the new composition over the Morph time, live and
  layer by layer, instead of snapping.
- **Animate Context's light** : draw a path on the light pad and play it, with the
  Metasurface's controls (direction, loop, time, jump, wiggle). It is saved with the
  session and recalled with scenes.
- **Surface material evolution** (Context) : a slider that keeps the surface from sitting
  perfectly still, as if a little wind moved the projector or the camera filming it. The
  material sways a few pixels in slow, irregular gusts, turns and breathes very slightly,
  and its light shifts with it. 0 (the default) is still. The section is now called
  **surface material** (plain "surface" also named the Metasurface).

### Resolume OSC mapper (new page, key `K`)

- Open a Resolume composition (`.avc`) : its layers, groups, columns, clips, effects and
  dashboard links become the columns of a matrix whose rows are Palinopsia's signals
  (modulators, Meta knobs, audio, vision and body features, any `/opsia` address).
- Click or paint to connect, set an amount per cell, float / toggle / trigger columns with
  range and smoothing, **Learn** addresses from Resolume's own OSC output, scene → column
  triggers, 8 snapshots and a **lock**. The whole mapping is saved with the session.

### Nodes and blend modes

- Five new native nodes, usable in any rack : **Remap**, **Luma Blur** (with a
  depth-of-field mode on the depth map), **Gooey** (blur then threshold), **Matte** (three
  inputs) and **Lookup** (a live layer as the palette). Nodes can now take a second layer
  input.
- **LighterColor** blend mode : the whole brighter pixel wins, hues never mix.
- **Weave, lumakey and consume** work as layer blend modes against the stack below :
  weave displaces the two by each other's brightness, lumakey keys out the layer's black
  background, and consume lets the layer and the stack fight over the frame.

## Fixed

### The picture and the engine

- **Always the fast graphics card.** On a laptop with two graphics chips, Windows could
  run Palinopsia on the slow integrated one (about 15x slower) unless you changed its
  graphics settings. It now asks for the discrete GPU by itself.
- **Fulldome no longer exhausts the graphics card.** Two blue screens (0x116
  VIDEO_TDR_FAILURE, the driver out of resources) during dome shows traced back to video
  memory : the main canvas carried a multisampled color buffer and a depth buffer it
  never used, hundreds of MB at a 4096² master. The dome now adds about 300 MB instead of
  750 (measured), the live master stops at 4K (8K alone added about 1.9 GB), the shared
  capture never exceeds 4096, and the engine skips a frame instead of queueing more work
  when the card falls two frames behind. A few small graphics-memory leaks are closed.
- **Palinopsia asks for the high-performance graphics card** on laptops with two.
- **Context at zero is exactly zero** : with no surface material, a hidden relief still ran
  on flat 8-bit maps and softened the whole picture slightly. Texture off is now an exact
  passthrough.
- **Surface materials** : seven non-square scans were stretched to about twice their
  height (see above), and **paper crumpled** repeated a streak of dark pits as vertical
  lines across the frame; both fixed.

- **Knobs no longer jump the picture.** Motion driven by a rate knob used to leap when the
  rate moved, so an LFO, a Morph or MIDI on a rate turned into strobing noise. Rates now
  accumulate smoothly on the layer's own clock (Speed, freeze and reverse still apply),
  across the Organic family and the glitch effects.
- **Long shows stay alive.** Glitch effects, grain and CRT / VHS parasites used to settle
  into fixed patterns after an hour or two; every generator still moves, unrepeating,
  after 24 hours.
- **Audio-reactive generators read the right data.** The waveform and spectrum could swap
  places, and the spectrum is now spread musically (30 Hz to 16 kHz) instead of bunching
  to one side. Any generator that listens gets the audio, on the background slab too.
- **Minimizing the window no longer stalls the show** (projector, Spout, NDI, Sonify and
  OSC out used to drop to about one frame a second).
- **Freeze-proofing** : switching a Parametric, Text or Collage slot away, or disposing a
  film, output-shape, flash-safety or depth-shadow stage, could freeze every shader draw.
  They all survive that, and a GPU reset, now.
- **Multi-pass effects** no longer see a black frame when they start (which could also
  stick in their memory).
- Fresh installs start with the intended defaults : Flash safety on at 0.35, the Flow macro
  neutral, Morph at 1 s, the audio monitor at 0.8 (they started off, fully engaged, at 0 ms
  and muted).

### Sources

- **All 23 generators** audited : each keeps moving after 24 hours (Slabs froze after
  three hours, Particle Drift, Ten Print and Ash fell into lattices and stripes); lines
  keep a steady pixel width; the aspect is right at 16:9 and on the square dome; presets
  set every input, so one no longer leaks into the next. Murmuration's birds no longer
  accelerate into strobing dots, Congeal fades back to black, and Metamorph, Differential,
  Direct Marks, Filaments, Column Scan, Shapes and Solid Color each lost a visible flaw.
- **Collage** : churn no longer collapses the wall onto one film, pieces stay sharp at 4K,
  crossfades and seeks no longer stick, Speed applies, and a minimized window no longer
  freezes the wall.
- **Text** : the weight dial works on every font, size and spacing move smoothly (a glyph
  atlas), stretch stays sharp, and a Text layer is no longer an opaque black slab.
- **Parametric** : Raster hears the audio, the spectrum uses its whole width (512 log
  columns), the spectrogram scrolls sharp at any frame rate, and Bars follow `scale`.
- **Mosaïque** matches tiles at last (its matcher never compiled).

### Effects and nodes

- **Context's Vibe Color button works** : it lights the scene in the color the Vibe
  Palette is painting with (a color chord's too), and turns the light up if it was off.
- **52 effects** audited. Highlights : texture reads the loader silently rewrote (Databend,
  Compress, Aperture, Optical Rain); eight effects that turned a transparent layer into a
  black slab; knobs that did nothing (Pixel Sort length, the Compress and Pixelmask grids,
  digital grain size); Rutt drew no lines in bright areas; Phosphene burned in for good;
  Distort's bulge and pinch were swapped; four time effects showed the previous frame;
  sizes now follow the frame height, so 4K and the dome keep the 1080p look. The
  corrupted-data smears, stepped blocks, CRT moiré and edge fringes stay the default look.
- **All 25 native nodes** : they keep the layer's transparency, run on the layer's clock
  (Speed, freeze and the background's slow clock), read the audio, and restart cleanly
  after PANIC. Feedback no longer lifts black into gray fog, Sediment's memory fades,
  Datamosh's mask clears, Réponse's gain works, Chronoscan shows the present at full
  resolution, Toile costs about 3 ms at 4K instead of 14. Their signature looks (Feedback's
  heat, Datamosh's melt, the Autocutter's torn paper) are kept.
- **Flash safety now does its job** : it counts real flashes per second in real light, per
  region of the frame. A 10 Hz strobe comes out clean at the default setting, and ordinary
  content passes through untouched.
- **The finishing chain** : Vibe no longer turns clipped highlights gray or black, Context no
  longer delays the whole master by a frame or leaves a permanent ghost, sharpening
  survives 3D, and torn VHS lines no longer smear the frame edge.
- **PANIC** also clears the effects' trail memories, which could hold a ghost for good.

### Video

- **Above 8× a clip froze** on real footage (each frame was an exact seek, 0.5 to 3 s on
  heavy 1080p or 4K), and below 1/16× it silently played at 1/16×. Both now play at their
  set rate (measured : 1/64, 1/28, 8 and 64).
- **Grain voices were upside down**, and while one loaded it could drop the whole grain
  picture for a few frames.
- **A paused video ignored seeks** (OSC `/video/position`, modulation) : it lands now.

### Output and recording

- **Changing sessions is a clean cut** : the previous session no longer keeps playing
  on the projector while the new one loads. The picture holds still until the new
  session is ready, then dissolves into it.
- **Recording can always be stopped** : a take the encoder refused used to hang the stop
  button. It now ends by itself and says why.
- **HIVE output** starts (it asked the hardware encoder for a frame rate it refuses).
- A recording that never received a frame no longer leaves an empty file behind.

### Randomize

- A pinned dice range keeps the current value instead of forcing one; the walk and
  Variation follow the same rules; palettes roll as one hue from near-black to pale
  instead of five clashing colors.

## Performance (measured at a 4K composition)

- Projector window open : 25 to 27 fps before, **60** now.
- Output page with the 4096² dome simulator : 47 to 49 fps before, **60** now.
- **Spout at 4K : 0 fps before**, about **58** now.
- Projector + Spout + a DXV3 take together : 57 to 60 fps.
- NDI 4096² at 30 fps, even minimized.
- Fulldome at 4096² : about **300 MB** of video memory instead of 750.
- Body tracking : about **half** the cost (once per camera frame).

## Known limits

- **Syphon** is built in CI but has not yet been tried on a Mac with a Syphon client. If it
  misbehaves, please open an issue.
- **NDI** is tested on Windows. The macOS and Linux paths are written but untested, the
  builds do not bundle the NDI runtime (use the one-click install, or NDI Tools), and on
  Linux the runtime has to be installed by hand.
- The hardware video encoder stops at 3840×2160 : larger takes (the 4K dome) record DXV3.

## For developers

- **tools/shader-rig** checks any generator or effect offscreen : a 24-hour show in one
  step, knob scrubs, 4K cost and stills. **tools/test-build.sh** runs an isolated copy of
  the app for automated tests, beside the real one.
- A shader can declare a **wrapped phase** (`uniform float PH_x; // wrap <period>`) to stay
  precise over day-long shows.
- **docs/bibliography.md** cites the research papers behind the instrument.
