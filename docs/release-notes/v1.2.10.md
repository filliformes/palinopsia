**Palinopsia v1.2.10 : the key moves on its own time.**

A release about the key sequencer and Sonify's controls. The key no longer changes
like a metronome : its free-time Every can be modulated and varied at random. Picture
can choose only among the keys you write. Sonify's modulation now works like the rest
of the app, with numbers that move with it, and the sequencers fold away.

## Highlights

- **The key sequencer stops keeping time like a metronome.** In free time, **every**
  can be bound to a modulator or a Meta knob (its M), and its number moves with it;
  **vary** draws each wait at random around it, from half to twice Every, or a quarter
  to four times at full. The wait running shows beside the key.
- **Picture on your own keys.** Picture can choose from the pool, as before, or from a
  **list** of up to 8 keys you write : the picture's color and light pick the nearest
  one. Its wheel shows your keys as dots.
- **Sonify's modulation works like the rest of the app.** Its M chips show which
  modulators are bound, open the same binding row as the Inspector right under the
  parameter (not at the top of the column any more), and clear with a right-click. A
  modulated slider's number now moves with the modulation.
- **The sequencers fold** to their headers, and keep playing.
- **The Modulation strip's count** of bindings sits under its name.

## Opening an older session

Everything opens as before : vary is off, Picture chooses from the pool, and the
sequencers are open. A binding made in Sonify swings around the value, as it always did.

## Install

| Platform | File |
|---|---|
| **Windows** | `Palinopsia-1.2.10-win-x64.exe` (installer) or `Palinopsia-1.2.10-portable.exe` |
| **macOS** (Apple Silicon) | `Palinopsia-1.2.10-mac-arm64.dmg` |
| **Linux** | `Palinopsia-1.2.10-linux-x86_64.AppImage` or `Palinopsia-1.2.10-linux-amd64.deb` |

Builds are unsigned :
- **Windows** : SmartScreen may warn, click **More info → Run anyway**.
- **macOS** (Apple Silicon) : not notarized. Drag Palinopsia into **Applications** first
  (run from the disk image or Downloads, macOS runs a hidden temporary copy), then run
  `xattr -cr /Applications/Palinopsia.app` once in Terminal, or open it and click **Open
  Anyway** in System Settings → Privacy & Security. The first launch asks for the local
  network, and for the camera and the microphone when you use them : accept, or OSC, NDI
  and light output stay silent. Screen capture is the one permission macOS never prompts
  for : switch Palinopsia on under Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording,
  then start it again.
- **Linux** : `chmod +x` the AppImage, then run it.

**Intel Macs (x64)** : this release ships Apple Silicon only. On an Intel Mac, build from
source (`npm install`, then `npm run build:mac`).

## For developers

- **A Sonify readout that follows modulation** : `LiveNum` (sonifyUi.tsx) on the shared
  live loop; a live-overlay subscriber can now take an `idle` callback, run on the frames
  nothing modulates it, to put the stored value back.
- **Sonify's M chip and binding row** are the Inspector's (`useBound`, `AssignRow`); a
  chip in a row of its own opens its row through the page's `assignBox(...params)`.
- **`keyEvery`** is a Sonify mod target whose base comes from the key sequencer
  (`registerKeyEveryBase`), on its slider's log map (`everyToPos` / `posToEvery`).
- **`Shell`** folds with `collapsed` / `onCollapse`.
