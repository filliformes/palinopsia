**Palinopsia v1.2.10** (in progress : the title comes with the release)

## Highlights

- **The key sequencer stops keeping time like a metronome.** In free time, **every**
  can be bound to a modulator or a Meta knob (its M), and its number moves with it;
  **vary** draws each wait at random around it, from half to twice Every, or a quarter
  to four times at full. The wait running shows beside the key.
- **Picture on your own keys.** Picture can choose from the pool, as before, or from a
  **list** of up to 8 keys you write : the picture's color and light pick the nearest
  one. Its wheel shows your keys as dots.
- **Sonify's modulation works like the rest of the app.** Its M chips show which
  modulators are bound, open the same binding row as the Inspector right under the
  parameter (not at the top of the column any more), and clear with a right-click. A
  modulated slider's number now moves with the modulation.
- **The sequencers fold** to their headers, and keep playing.
- **The Modulation strip's count** of bindings sits under its name.

## Opening an older session

Everything opens as before : vary is off, Picture chooses from the pool, and the
sequencers are open. A binding made in Sonify swings around the value, as it always did.
