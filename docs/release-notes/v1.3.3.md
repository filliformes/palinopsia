**Palinopsia v1.3.3** (in progress)

## New

- **Lights : your DMX lights in Palinopsia.** A new page (key `T`, or the **LIGHTS**
  button at the top of the Output page) for LED bars, spots and LED matrices. Place each one on the picture, over the
  part it should take its light from : drag it and it takes its color from somewhere
  else. Each light can show the whole picture, one layer, a color or a gradient running
  across the room, with an effect on top (a chase, waves, a pulse, sparkle, the sound,
  the beat), and a **tuning** to match it to the real light. It comes with **shows** that
  fit any room, **light scenes** of their own, and a lighting console : a grand master,
  speed, fades and **Black**. Out through an **Enttec DMX USB Pro** (found by itself, and
  back by itself after it is unplugged or the computer sleeps), **Art-Net**, **sACN** or
  **WLED**. Every slider can be modulated, the console learns MIDI, and OSC reaches it
  all. The old light output of the Output page comes over as a matrix.
  - **Smooth fades on real lights.** A light with a dimmer channel now gets its level
    on the dimmer and its color at full strength, in 16 bits when it has a fine channel :
    fades stay smooth down low, and a dim color stays its color.
  - **The picture's colors, not its beige.** A light can read its part of the picture
    as the average, the most vivid color or the brightest; **from the picture** (and the
    **Picture colors** show) makes a gradient of the picture's main colors.
  - **Groups.** Pick several lights (Shift or Ctrl + click) and make them a group : a
    fader of their own on the console, a look or a show for them alone.
  - **Real lights, by name.** Choose the kind of light from a list, or import its file
    from the Open Fixture Library : its channels come in the right order, with the
    shutter held open and everything it does not use left alone.
  - **In time with the projector.** Each output can wait a few milliseconds so the lights
    land with the projected picture (about 40 ms); Art-Net can send ArtSync.
- **dataflou : see what flows.** The dataflou section of the Connect tab shows what comes
  in and goes out, live : each value with where it comes from or goes, its last seconds as
  a small line, and how many a second.

## Fixed

- **dataflou comes back by itself.** When a computer of the mesh went to sleep or lost its
  network, the connection stayed lost until Max (or the other app) was closed and opened
  again. Now Palinopsia calls the lost node back by itself, a few seconds after it can be
  reached again, and calls everyone again as soon as its own computer wakes up or changes
  network.
