**Palinopsia v1.3.3 : the Lights.**

A release about light in the room. A new **Lights** page brings DMX lights into
Palinopsia : LED bars, spots, rings and LED panels, each placed on the picture over the
part it takes its colors from, so the room plays with the projection. Plug in an
**Enttec DMX USB Pro**, follow the five steps of the **Set up** guide, and your lights are
playing in minutes; Art-Net, sACN and WLED work too. Shows, cues, groups and a lighting
console come with it. And dataflou stays connected : a computer that sleeps or drops off
the network comes back by itself, and you can now see the data flowing in and out.

## New

### Lights

- **A page for your lights.** Press `T`, or **LIGHTS** at the top of the Output page. Add
  a bar, a spot, a ring or a panel, and drag it on the map over the part of the picture it
  should take its light from : each LED shows, live, the color it sends. A light can show
  the whole picture, one layer, a color or a gradient running across the room, or nothing,
  with an effect on top, and a **tuning** (brightness, contrast, gamma, saturation, hue,
  warmth) to match it to the real light.

#### Setting up

- **From nothing to light in five steps.** With nothing selected, the right of the page
  walks you through it : plug in the Enttec, say what kind of lights you have and how
  many, set on each light the address the page shows, run the test, pick a show. The
  steps tick as they are done, and you can come back to them any time.

- **Your kind of light, by name.** Pick it from the list (pixel strips, LED bars, RGB
  and RGBW lights, dimmer and strobe lights, white lights, panels) or import its file from
  the Open Fixture Library : its channels come in the right order, the shutter held open
  and everything it does not use left alone. The 1 m RGB pixel bars of 30 zones are in the
  list, mode by mode.

- **Your lights find themselves (RDM).** The page asks the lights on the DMX line what
  they are, and lists each one with its mode and its address. Make one blink to find it in
  the room, set its address and its mode from the page instead of its little menu, give
  them all addresses in a row, and put them on the map in one press, all through the
  Enttec DMX USB Pro. Lights that do not speak RDM simply do not answer : set those by hand.

- **A test that tells.** **test** sweeps a line across the room in red, green, blue and
  white : a light lit in the wrong color has another kind, a dark one has the wrong
  address.

- **A patch you can see.** Every universe at a glance, each light in its color, the
  channels two lights share in red. Drag a light to readdress it, or **address in order**.
  The DIP switches of each address are drawn for the lights that have them.

- **Any shape.** Bend a bar where it bends in the room (double-click it), lay out LED
  rings, and wire a panel any of the 16 ways panels are wired. Pick several lights (Shift
  or Ctrl + click, or a rubber band), add a row of N in one go, and line them up, spread
  them or mirror them.

- **Your room under the map.** Place the lights over the picture, a plain grid, or a
  photo of the room (or the stage plan) so each one sits where it really is.

#### Playing

- **The picture's colors, not its beige.** A light reads its part of the picture as the
  average, the most vivid color or the brightest; **from the picture** turns the picture's
  main colors into a gradient across the room.

- **Effects that play like a console.** Chase, wave, pulse, sparkle, the sound (all of
  it, or the lows, the mids or the highs : the floor spots on the kick, the bars on the
  cymbals), the beat, and **presence** : the lights near the people in the room light up.
  An effect can travel through the room or light by light (left to right, from the center,
  at random, odd and even), folded in wings, with a fan of colors across the room. Effects
  never flash more than three times a second.

- **Digital effects.** The lights become a small computer of their own : an afterimage of
  what the picture just was, an echo of it seconds ago, sample and hold, eight colors,
  glitch, a tape head sliding through the picture, cellular automata, the Game of Life,
  fireflies falling into step, clouds, the beats counted in binary.

- **Smooth fades on real lights.** A light with a dimmer gets its level on the dimmer and
  its color at full strength, in 16 bits when it has a fine channel : fades stay smooth
  down low, and a dim color stays its color.

- **Groups.** Lights that play together : a fader of their own, a knob on the console,
  and a grade (brightness, contrast, saturation, hue, warmth) over the whole group.

- **The console.** A grand master, the speed of every effect, brightness, saturation and
  contrast for every light, the cue fade, and **Black** (every light down, and back up).
  Every knob learns MIDI, every slider with an **M** takes a modulator or a Macro knob, and
  OSC reaches it all. The top bar's **■ Black** takes the lights down with the show.

#### Shows

- **27 shows that fit any room.** Each one a look for every light at once, fading in,
  with a swatch of what it looks like. Their patterns run across the space, so each light
  takes its part from where it stands. **Classic** : the picture, each its layer, the
  picture's colors, warm white, sunset, ocean, fire, rainbow drift, chases, on the beat,
  sound reactive, breathing, night sparkle, dark. **Digital** : afterimage, echo, sample
  and hold, eight colors, glitch, tape head, Rule 30, triangles, the Game of Life,
  fireflies, cloud field, binary count.

#### Cues

- **A cue list for the lights.** Save what the lights show as a cue, then **GO** and
  **back** through them. Each cue can have its own fade, a follow time so the cues run by
  themselves, and a Palinopsia scene it goes with : recall the scene and its lights come
  with it. The cues are a lane of the Seq page too, and GO, back and follow learn MIDI.

#### Setups

- **Light setups.** Keep a whole setup (the lights and where they are, the outputs, the
  groups, what they show) by name, or as a file to take to another computer, and switch
  between them under the same project : one piece, several rooms. The setup you leave is
  kept first, so nothing is lost.

#### Outputs

- **Out to the lights.** An **Enttec DMX USB Pro** is found by itself, and comes back by
  itself when it is unplugged and plugged in again or the computer wakes up. **Art-Net**,
  **sACN** and **WLED** go over the network, and **find** lists the controllers there so
  you pick one instead of typing its address.

- **In time with the projector.** Each output can wait a few milliseconds so the lights
  land with the projected picture (about 40 ms); Art-Net can send ArtSync so every
  universe changes at the same moment.

- **Dark when it stops.** A light that stops being fed (sending off, its output removed,
  the app quitting) gets one last dark frame, so the room never stays lit in its last look.

### dataflou

- **See what flows.** The dataflou section of the Connect tab shows what comes in and
  goes out, live : each value with where it comes from or goes, its last seconds as a
  small line, and how many arrive a second.

- **Scenes of connections.** Palinopsia takes part in dataflou's new scenes : a cue
  (**store n**, **recall n**) from any node, Max or the visualizer's **scenes** panel saves
  or brings back every node's own connections, 16 slots. A computer away during a recall
  catches up when it comes back.

- **The newest mesh visualizer.** The page Palinopsia opens is dataflou's latest : the
  scenes panel, this computer's address, a universe selector (it moves Palinopsia's node
  too), undo and redo.

### Macro

- **See what the knobs drive.** The Macro row's title bar shows a capsule for every
  destination, knob by knob, like the Modulation row's : point at one and its knob
  lights up, **×** takes it off. They stay in view with the row folded.

- **A range on every knob.** Under the curve, a min and a max : a full turn of the knob
  sweeps only that part of each destination's range (min above max turns it the other
  way). Drag the ends of the bar or type the numbers; double-click for the full range.
  OSC : `/opsia/macro/n/min` and `/max`.

## Changed

- **The lights moved out of the Output page.** The Output page's light section is now the
  Lights page, opened from the **LIGHTS** button in its header (lit while the lights
  send). The bottom bar stays as it was.

## Fixed

- **Sliders follow the Macro knobs.** A slider a Macro knob drives now moves as you turn
  the knob (or as MIDI, OSC or a glide moves it), like a modulated one, instead of
  jumping at the end.

- **dataflou comes back by itself.** When a computer of the mesh went to sleep or lost its
  network, the connection stayed lost until the other app was closed and opened again.
  Now Palinopsia calls the lost computer back by itself, a few seconds after it can be
  reached again, and calls everyone again as soon as its own computer wakes up or changes
  network.

## Opening an older session

Everything opens as before. The light grid of the old Output page comes over once, as a
panel on its own output, and in a session saved before every light follows the picture.

## Install

| Platform | File |
|---|---|
| **Windows** | `Palinopsia-1.3.3-win-x64.exe` (installer) or `Palinopsia-1.3.3-portable.exe` |
| **macOS** (Apple Silicon) | `Palinopsia-1.3.3-mac-arm64.dmg` |
| **Linux** | `Palinopsia-1.3.3-linux-x86_64.AppImage` or `Palinopsia-1.3.3-linux-amd64.deb` |

Builds are unsigned :
- **Windows** : SmartScreen may warn, click **More info → Run anyway**.
- **macOS** (Apple Silicon) : not notarized. Drag Palinopsia into **Applications** first,
  then run `xattr -cr /Applications/Palinopsia.app` once in Terminal, or open it and click
  **Open Anyway** in System Settings → Privacy & Security. The first launch asks for the
  local network, and for the camera and the microphone when you use them : accept, or
  OSC, NDI, Ableton Link and the network lights stay silent. Screen capture is the one
  permission macOS never prompts for : switch Palinopsia on under Privacy & Security →
  Screen & System Audio Recording, then start it again.
- **Linux** : `chmod +x` the AppImage, then run it.

**Intel Macs (x64)** : this release ships Apple Silicon only. On an Intel Mac, build from
source : `npm install`, `npm run fetch:depth`, `npm run fetch:pose`, then
`npm run build:mac`.

## For developers

- **The Lights** : the model in `shared/lights.ts` (the rig is this computer's, the looks,
  cues and masters the session's), the built-in kinds in `shared/lightProfiles.ts`, the
  engine and the digital effects in `renderer/src/lights/` (`lightEngine.ts`,
  `lightFx.ts`, `lightShows.ts`, `ofl.ts`), the Enttec over Web Serial in
  `lights/enttec.ts`, the network outputs in `main/light.ts` (`DmxSender`), the page in
  `components/LightsPage.tsx`. OSC under `/opsia/lights/...` (see the OSC reference).
- **dataflou** : the redial of lost nodes and the per-path counts behind the data view
  live in `src/main/dataflou`.
