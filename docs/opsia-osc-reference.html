Palinopsia · Control Surface

Opsia / OSC Reference

Every knob, slab and macro in the instrument answers to an OSC address. This is the full /opsia/… namespace — how Pandore, TouchDesigner, or any OSC source plays Opsia, and how Opsia answers back: mirroring its state, streaming the analysed picture over /opsia/vision, and firing embodied gestures over /body.

namespace /opsia/… transport UDP values 0 – 1 normalized indices 1-based discovery OSCQuery : port+1
00

Reading the map

one write path, uniform units

Continuous controls take a single normalized float, rescaled to the target's declared range on arrival — so a controller never needs to know a parameter's real min/max. A handful of address shapes cover everything else.

Continuous
One float 0..1, scaled into the declared range of the target.
Enum
An int index (from 0), a float 0..1 across the members, or the member's name string. The OSC type tag decides : a float 2.0 is the top of the range, not index 2.
Boolean
True at ≥ 0.5 (mute · solo · feedback · isolate).
Trigger
Fires on the rising edge — send 1, then 0 to re-arm.
Color
3–4 raw args r g b [a], or a single value read as grayscale.
Point2D
Two normalized args, each scaled to its own axis range.
Indices
Layers, Meta knobs, scenes, audio bands, Worlds and FX slots count from 1, like the UI. Enum indices count from 0.
Tempo
/opsia/bpm is sent in BPM, clamped 20..800. The few other raw values (seconds, gains, a pan, a count) say so in their row.
Outside /opsia
Only /opsia/… plays Opsia. While the Resolume mapper learns, Resolume's own /composition/… messages become its columns; anything else is ignored.

Sources & FX racks

slot = A | B · i = 1-based

Each layer has two source slots and three FX racks — one per source, plus the mixed layer rack. Any shader input is addressable by its declared name; the value auto-scales to that input's type and range (including color and point2D multi-arg forms).

AddressArgDoes
…/layer{n}/source/{slot}sSwap the source generator : a shaderId (a shader of your own from the Source Editor goes by its user-… id), a built-in's display name, or none. The one already there keeps its settings. none empties the slot; a name it does not know leaves the slot as it is.
…/source/{slot}/{input}f 0..1 →A source shader input, scaled to its range.
…/source/{slot}/fx/{i}/{input}f 0..1 →An input on the per-source FX unit i.
…/layer{n}/fx/{i}/{input}f 0..1 →An input on the per-layer FX unit i.

A/B Harmony & Coupling

per layer · the Mixer

How a layer's B answers its A : in color (the harmony) and to the sound (the coupling, driven by the audio tab's input). Both sit in the Mixer's columns.

AddressArgDoes
…/layer{n}/harmonyf 0..1The A/B harmony, B's colors relative to A's (the Mixer's HUE, -1..1 there) : 0 pulls B onto A's colors, 0.5 leaves B as it is, 1 puts B on A's complement.
/opsia/coupling≥ 0.5The master switch for every layer's coupling (the Mixer header's ≈ coupling). On at launch, not saved.
…/layer{n}/coupling/modei · f · sBond character : off · lean · hocket · cut · gate · drift.
…/layer{n}/coupling/amountf 0..1Coupling depth.
…/layer{n}/coupling/tightnessf 0..1Vestigial (0) ↔ obvious (1).
…/layer{n}/coupling/featurei 0..6 · f · sThe audio feature that drives it : level · flux · transient · centroid · band · pitch · noisiness. An index (0 to 6) or a name reaches all seven; a float 0..1 spreads over the first six, level to pitch, as it always did, so noisiness takes index 6 or its name. Advertised and mirrored as a whole number 0..6.
…/layer{n}/coupling/bandi 0..5 · f 0..1Which of the six bands the band feature listens to : a whole number, 0 the lowest to 5 the highest, or a fraction 0..1 across the six. A whole number counts as the band whatever its type tag (a float 1.0 is band 1, the second). Advertised and mirrored as a whole number 0..5, so a fader built from the advertisement reaches every band.

Audio sensors

inbound only · Pandore → Opsia

The audio brain pushes analysis features straight to the bus, where audio modulators and the A/B coupling read them per frame. They bypass the parameter store : sensor inputs, not controls, never echoed back on the feedback stream. They count while the audio tab's source is OSC, or Both (OSC wins while it is fresher than half a second, else the local input plays).

Sonify

image → sound · v = voice

The image-to-sound engine speaks its own sub-namespace. The eleven voices are spectra · orbit · flow · events · raster · sstv · filter · signal · chord · collage · assemblage (sstv is the Transmission voice); each takes the same shape of address, so a controller can play any voice's probes, pitch and timbre. The patch also travels with sessions and scenes. Index addresses (the root octave, a reading path, the orbit shape, the chord's voice count, a mode, an octave, a tap, a sync mode, the voicing) are advertised and sent as integers and read raw; a float sent there is read as 0..1 across the range. A NaN is read as 0.

/opsia/sonify/root · rootoct · scalei · sGlobal quantizer — key root, root octave, and scale : a name (harmonic minor, dimwh, Diminished W-H…) or an index : 0 chromatic · 1 major · 2 minor · 3 minor pentatonic · 4 whole tone · 5 dorian · 6 phrygian · 7 lydian · 8 harmonic minor · 9 ionian · 10 mixolydian · 11 aeolian · 12 locrian · 13 major pentatonic · 14 major blues · 15 minor blues · 16 diminished W-H · 17 diminished H-W.

/opsia/sonify/keyseq/modes · iHow the next key is chosen : list · circle · affinity · pivot · picture.

Vision

outbound only · the picture answers back

The composited output is analysed back into features and streamed out (when OSC-out is on), so the image itself can play a sound brain — the inward half of the loop. Each is a single 0..1 float; bind them on the far side as you would any control. The Performance tab's picture section shows them all live, with what 0 and 1 mean.

AddressArgDoes
/opsia/vision/{feature}f 0..1 →One picture feature — brightness · contrast · motion · edges · entropy · centroidX · centroidY · warmth · saturation · hue · depth · depthSpread · flowX · flowY · divergence · curl · coherence (the last five are the picture's motion field : which way it flows, whether it spreads out or closes in, whether it turns, and whether it moves as one).
/opsia/body/{feature}f 0..1 →One body feature, streamed while the Body camera runs : hands, pose, face, the nine silhouette zones, presence, the camera's motion field flowEnergy · flowX · flowY · flowDivergence · flowCurl · flowCoherence · flowCenterX · flowCenterY · flowArea, and the movement qualities moveEnergy · moveExpansion · moveFluidity · moveSuddenness · moveDirectness · moveSymmetry.

Embodied · Body

outbound only · under /body/, not /opsia

The Body page's gesture rules can fire their own OSC. When a rule fires and its OSC out is on, Opsia sends a bang to the OSC-out target — so a body gesture (a pinch, a pose, a shadow covering a screen zone, someone walking into frame) can play the sound side. These live under /body/…, outside the /opsia namespace, with the name you gave the rule.

AddressArgDoes
/body/{name}i 1A gesture rule fired — the custom OSC name, or a truncated default like /body/HandsUpRndMod.
14

Discovery & feedback

two-way sync

Opsia doesn't just receive — it describes itself and mirrors its live state, so a controller's surface can auto-bind and stay in lock-step as modulators sweep, scenes recall, or a hand moves a slider. Three routes, one shared address space.

OSCQuery · HTTP

The fixed controls, self-describing (type · range · value · description) : index addresses typed i, the picture's features read-only. Shader inputs on sources and FX racks are not listed (address them by name); the three finishing units' and the Background ground's float inputs are.

On this computer only (it listens on 127.0.0.1; on port-1 when the OSC port is 65535). The tree follows what is loaded : while OSC in listens, it is published again (at most twice a second) whenever the rows change, a source or an effect loaded, a slot's film or sound, the Audio Layer's slots, the ground's inputs, the Body camera and its features.

http://127.0.0.1:port+1/ · ?HOST_INFO

OSCQuery · WebSocket

A live value-stream on the same port for web / no-UDP clients, every 100 ms while one is attached. With no LISTEN a client gets every path; LISTEN narrows it to the paths named, IGNORE drops one. Triggers, seeks, the audio sensors and /opsia/audiolayer/play are never streamed.

→ {COMMAND:"LISTEN", DATA:"/opsia/…"}
← {FULL_PATH, VALUE:[v]}

Outbound feedback

A throttled diff-and-send loop (every 100 ms) pushes only changed controls back to a configured host:port : the same /opsia/… addresses and values as OSCQuery advertises, indices as ints. It follows what is loaded live. Triggers, seeks, the audio sensors and /opsia/audiolayer/play are never echoed. Pandore's UI tracks Opsia's.

Opsia → UDP → host:port

MARK · the picture as sound

A scanline of the output, drawn like an optical soundtrack : /opsia/av/mark-signal (32 floats) with mark-level · mark-centroid · mark-flux, to the same host:port.

MARK on, in the OSC section's ↗ out

The OSC monitor (the OSC section's monitor button) shows every message in and out, live, with its address, values and destination.

The same addresses are played by two other routes : the dataflou mesh (each of its destinations is an /opsia/… address, played once per change) and the Agent (MCP)'s osc tool (/opsia/… only; a trigger it sends fires every time). The agent's numbers go as 0..1 floats, as on the wire, so 1 is the top of a menu; an int goes only to an index address, or as {"int": n}.
