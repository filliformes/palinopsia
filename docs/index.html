Palinopsia · Opsia · a visual instrument

A visual instrument you play.

Palinopsia is a WebGL2 compositor you perform, not a tool you wire. It stacks layers of generated and treated video, masters the whole picture through a fixed set of stages, and lets you steer every parameter live: by hand, by an internal modulation brain, over OSC and MIDI, from the other instruments of a dataflou mesh, or with your own body.

Free and open source, for Windows, macOS and Linux. The name is the persistence of a visual image after the stimulus is gone.

What it can make

Every frame below was built by the app alone, no external footage. Pick a recipe from Generate and it composes the whole session for you.

New in 1.2.5

Data and rhythm. A source of machine-cut marks, a sound voice that reads the picture as data, a Vary for every layer, and the readout that used to stall the frame rate made free.

  • Lowercase, a new source : machine-cut marks on a stepped clock. A data matrix, blocks, bars, ticks, a scan rule, a lattice, sprocket ladders and a slab with bars cut out of it, landing on a step and holding. It pulses on its own, or plays any rhythm you bind to its gate, and it can read another layer as a 1-bit halftone of cells. At 512 cells the slab is a barcode.
  • Signal, a new Sonify voice S : read heads walk the picture on a clock and every bright cell they cross fires a micro-grain, from a 0.2 ms tick to a 500 ms ring. The picture is the pattern; point it at Lowercase and the marks play themselves. Up to four heads drift in and out of step, and every grain lands on its exact sample.
  • A Vary for each layer, under its Speed : the same nudge as the global Vary, aimed at one layer, and a modulation target, so a pattern can fire it.
  • Re-deal one rack from a layer's right-click menu : the effects of source A, of source B, or the layer's own, and nothing else. Dealt racks now reach four effects.
  • You see what fires. Every Vary, the Randomize button and the return-to-default buttons blink when they act, however they were triggered.
  • A free picture readout. The vision features, Sonify's taps and the depth input used to stop the graphics chip for about 17 ms each read on a Mac. They are queued now, and on an Apple-silicon Mac Mini a session with the Performance tab open went from as low as 42 to a steady 50 frames on a 50 Hz display.
  • The frame rate reads against your display (50 fps / 50 Hz), dropped frames are counted, and the GPU and VRAM meters work on a Mac.
  • Esc closes a fullscreen output, so one display can never trap you, and screen capture on a Mac says how to grant its permission instead of showing black.

The full list is in the release notes.

New in 1.2

The dome and the grain. The biggest release yet: the picture goes under a dome and over the network, the camera reads movement, the sound gets a key of its own, and every change now morphs.

  • Fulldome output. A square domemaster (2K or 4K, 180 to 230°) feeding the projector, NDI, Spout, Syphon and recording, with a 3D dome simulator to check it from the seat or from outside.
  • NDI in and out, built in. Any NDI source on the network plays in a layer like a webcam, and Palinopsia is its own NDI source, 4096² at 30 fps. No NDI yet? One click installs the runtime.
  • Real-time DXV3 recording, the GPU compressing each frame as it goes, with the Sonify sound when it plays. Syphon on macOS, the twin of Spout.
  • Every change morphs. Scene recalls, Randomize, the sequencer, sessions, New and Generate move into the next composition over the Morph time, live and layer by layer, with no flick.
  • A dataflou node. Palinopsia joins the dataflou mesh: the other instruments on the network see what it senses and what plays it, and wire to it both ways, no central computer. A monitor shows every node and connection.
  • Resolume OSC mapper K. Open a Resolume composition and wire Palinopsia's signals to its layers, clips and effects on a pin board, with snapshots, a lock and a dice.
  • The camera reads movement. A motion field, six movement qualities of a body (energy, fluidity, suddenness…), and a Silhouette source that cuts the body out of the room, no green screen. Tracking runs on its own thread.
  • Living surfaces. New Organic generators that grow, crack, drift and heal (Grown, Colony, Ground, Scan), and the whole family lit as a relief.
  • Film damage, rebuilt from real film: dust, fibers, scratches and a hair in the gate per gauge, plus a dirt burst you can fire on a beat.
  • A key sequencer for Sonify composes the root and scale of every voice over time (a list, the circle of fifths, shared notes, a pivot, the picture's color), each mode drawn as it thinks, with a glide between keys.
  • Hear the Collage. A new Sonify voice plays the wall's films at once, each piece placed in the stereo field and tuned to your key, through a Ring bank inspired by the S-4. And a real level meter, in decibels.
  • Generate builds scenes that move: 100 recipes using the whole instrument, with Dome, Film Wall and Node Workshop families and 25 new master chains.
  • Installations look after themselves: start with the computer, heal a crashed or frozen picture, reopen a closed output, reconnect the camera and the sound card, keep a log.
  • The Background reads like the stack. It comes first in the Layers column (Background, then Layers 1 to 4, bottom to top), folds like a layer, and its group mode lets it show through the layers' dark parts; Generate and New let the ground show.
  • A Picture readout under Performance : every feature the picture reports (light, color, texture, motion, depth), live, in plain words.
  • An OSC monitor shows every message in and out, live, and effects travel between racks with a drag. Sonify's output device sits in the audio tab too.

Your first five minutes

  1. You land on a fresh, random one-layer scene. Press R a few times. Each press re-rolls the whole instrument (sources, effects, blends, modulators) from curated ranges and crossfades over the morph time. This is the fastest way to feel the range of the thing.
  2. Open the Feel tab with G and drag Density, Flow and Drift. These are whole-image macros: one slider reshapes the entire picture at once. Double-click any slider to reset it to neutral.
  3. Click a layer's source (the A chip) and play with its parameters in the Inspector under the preview. Every slider carries a dice for a curated random value, a preset menu, and an M button to attach a modulator.
  4. Keep something you like: click + in the scene bank to save it, then press 1–9 to recall your scenes live. Recalls morph over the Morph time, live: set it long and one scene slowly becomes the next.
  5. Go big: press O for the Output page, pick a display, and go fullscreen. Or reach for Generate in the toolbar: 100 recipes, each building a whole coherent session (visuals, palette, world and feel) in one click.
Lost at any point? Press ? for the full keyboard cheat-sheet, in the app. Esc closes it.

Beyond the picture

Palinopsia is one instrument with several rooms. Each is a full-page mode reached by a single key.

SonifyS

The image itself synthesizes audio in real time, inside the app, no external software. Ten voices (a scanning spectrogram, the frame as an oscillator, grain clouds off motion, struck edges, raster audification, SSTV, resonant filters, read heads that play the picture as data, a chord bank, and a Collage's own films heard as a tuned sound collage through a Ring bank inspired by the S-4) with a shared reverb/delay tail, an effects sequencer, and a key sequencer that composes the key of the whole instrument over time.

SequencerQ

An auto-pilot over your scene bank. Tag each scene, then let it wander: weighted or climate-shaped selection, morph or cut transitions, and slow durational long-forms (Burial, Long-Take, Frame-Weave) that reshape the whole run over minutes.

Modulation brainD

Eight modulators (LFOs, envelopes, chaos, Euclidean and Turing patterns, audio and vision followers, a body follower) and sixteen meta knobs write on top of your values every frame. Dial a value by hand, then let a shape move it. Everything answers to MIDI Learn and OSC too.

Output & mappingO

Fullscreen to any display, projection-map with a keystone editor, and record the take in real time, DXV3 included (it keeps rolling when you leave the page). Send over NDI, Spout, Syphon or HIVE (and play any NDI source on the network as a layer), go under a fulldome with a 3D simulator, span a custom composition across several projectors, run it as a self-healing installation, and push the picture's color into the room over DMX / WLED, all behind a built-in flash-safety limiter.

Resolume mapperK

Open a Resolume composition and its layers, clips, effects and dashboard become columns of a pin board whose rows are Palinopsia's signals: modulators, Meta knobs, the audio, picture and body features, any OSC address. Click to connect, set an amount, smooth it, and recall scenes as Resolume columns. 8 snapshots, a lock, and a dice for happy accidents.

dataflou meshA

Turn on the dataflou section of the audio/midi/osc tab and Palinopsia becomes a node of the mesh its sibling instruments speak. Nodes on the network find each other on their own, show what they publish and what they take, and wire any source to any destination, both ways. Palinopsia shares what it senses (sound, picture, body, modulators) and takes Meta knobs, layers, the feel macros, scenes and tempo.

Body B reads the room: turn a webcam on (opt-in) and MediaPipe reads your hands, pose, face and silhouette into the instrument. Route any tracked feature, any movement quality or the motion field into a modulator, or author gesture rules that fire actions and OSC: cover a screen zone with your shadow, or trigger on presence when someone walks into frame. Assemble E is a concatenative video editor: point it at a folder of films and it edits a matching sequence live, as a layer source. And Collage is a source: a wall of films cut up like torn paper, every piece its own film, that you can modulate, run through effects and hear in Sonify.

The mental model

A few ideas that make everything else click into place.

  • The picture is a stack, built bottom to top: a Background, then Layer 1 to Layer 4. The Layers column reads in that order, top down. The layers either blend onto the Background (Layer 1 in its own blend mode) or sit over it as a group, letting it through their dark parts.
  • Each layer holds two sources (A and B), a mix between them, its own effect racks, a blend mode onto the layers below, a spatial mask, and its own opacity and speed. A source is a generator, an imported video, a live capture, or a network stream.
  • Three stages master the whole picture, always last and always on: Vibe Palette (colour), Context (depth and atmosphere), and the Finalizer (final grade, grain, and output shape).
  • Anything you can reach, you can automate. The modulation brain and the meta knobs write on top of your values every frame, and the same targets answer to MIDI and OSC. You dial in a value by hand, then let a shape move it.

That is the instrument. The long list of sources, effects and nodes is vocabulary you learn over time. The shape of the thing does not change.

How do I…?

Get sound out of it?

Press S for Sonify. The image itself synthesizes audio in real time, inside the app, no external software. The probes you see drawn on the picture are the instrument.

Make a collage of my films?

Pick Collage as a layer source and open a folder in the Inspector: every piece plays its own film. Bind a modulator to cuts and the wall grows and shrinks a piece at a time; turn on FX before shapes to run the source's effects on the films while the seams and torn paper stay crisp; and turn on Sonify's Collage voice to hear them, tuned to your key.

Move an effect from a layer to the master?

Drag its chip onto the other rack (hold Ctrl to copy instead), or right-click it for Copy to Master FX / Copy to Layer N FX. Its modulation follows a move. A rack that can't host an effect says why.

Change scenes or sessions during a show without a jump?

Set the Morph time in the transport. Scene recalls, Randomize, the sequencer, loading a session, New and Generate all move into the next composition over that time, live: the same sources ease their every value, anything else crossfades layer by layer, and a new layer only fades in once it is ready. At Morph 0 a session holds the picture still while it compiles, then dissolves.

Use a MIDI controller?

Press L to arm MIDI Learn from anywhere, click a control (it pulses), then move a knob or hit a pad. It stays armed, so you can map the next control right away.

Play it with my body?

Press B for Body. Turn a webcam on (opt-in, off by default) and MediaPipe reads your hands, pose, face and silhouette: route any tracked feature into a modulator, or author gesture rules that fire actions and OSC. Cover a screen zone with your shadow to trigger it, or fire on presence (someone walking into or out of frame) for an installation.

Drive it from another app?

Every parameter is an OSC endpoint (advertised over OSCQuery), so a companion brain like dataFLOU or hardware like Pandore can play the whole instrument. The picture also streams back out over OSC, so the image can answer the sound. The OSC monitor shows every message both ways, live.

Wire it to my other instruments on the network?

Turn on the dataflou section in the audio/midi/osc tab (pick a name and a universe). Every dataflou node on the network appears there with its parameters: wire one of theirs to Palinopsia, or Palinopsia's sound, picture or body features to one of theirs, and watch every connection on the mesh. Wires come back on their own when a node reappears.

Drive Resolume with it?

Press K for the Resolume mapper, open your composition (.avc), and pin Palinopsia's signals to Resolume's controls. Turn on OSC input in Resolume's preferences; the Resolume section of the audio/midi/osc tab says when it is sending.

Project in a dome?

On the Output page, turn on Fulldome: Palinopsia renders a square domemaster (front at the bottom), fits a flat picture as full dome, panorama or screen, and shows it in a 3D dome simulator. Everything downstream (projector, NDI, Spout, Syphon, recording) gets the dome.

Go fullscreen or record a take?

Press O for the Output page: pick a display, set the resolution, and record (DXV3 records in real time, even the 4096² dome master). The take keeps rolling if you leave the page, so you can tweak parameters mid-recording, and it carries the Sonify sound when it plays.

Undo a mistake, or calm a runaway feedback?

Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z undo up to 100 steps. Press 0 to flush every self-feeding buffer at once, or H to freeze the output on the current frame while you sort things out.
